A Skill Passport supports a broader presentation of credentials, skills, projects and experiences alongside that transcript." - question: "Is every item in a Skill Passport institution-verified?" answer: "No. Institution-issued evidence, learner-added evidence and attested evidence remain distinct. Issued credentials retain their issuer context; learner additions remain learner contributions; attestations identify who reviewed or supported a particular item. Verification applies at item level." - question: "What can learners include in their Skill Passport?" answer: "Learners can connect credentials, skills, projects, experiences, achievements and supporting evidence. The evidence remains connected to its source and applicable verification resources." - question: "What does an attestation establish?" answer: "An attestation records a named educator's, mentor's or other attester's review of a specific achievement or evidence item. Its meaning follows the attester's role and the scope of that review; it does not automatically turn every learner contribution into an institution-issued credential." - question: "How does the Skill Passport support a lifelong learner journey?" answer: "A Skill Passport is designed to grow across education, projects, work and continuing learning.

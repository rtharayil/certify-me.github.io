name: E-learning program positioning
description: Owner-defined buyers and LMS relationship for the e-learning solution page.
---

The e-learning solution is for people running large learning programs: Program Managers, Credential Managers and Directors/Heads of Learning, with learning-experience, digital-learning and credentialing leaders. It is not primarily for generic learners, individual instructors or small course creators.

CertifyMe is the connected achievement infrastructure around an existing LMS, not a replacement LMS. The six-layer architecture connects completion with credentials, verification, reviewed skills, learner records and workforce value.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested an ICP and architectural rebuild of the e-learning solution, not merely a visual redesign.

**How to apply:** Keep the buyer story focused on multi-program operations, governance, engagement and outcomes. Learner sharing, portfolios and Skill Passport support that organizational story.

The owner identifies www.skillstory.org as “our website” and authorizes its publicly available content and visual assets as source material for CertifyMe’s Skill Passport solution page.

**Why:** The owner wants the page grounded in the existing SkillStory product experience, not an invented Skill Passport interface or replacement AI illustrations.

**How to apply:** Research the current SkillStory product pages and reuse its relevant original product visuals with documented provenance. Preserve the CertifyMe / SkillStory relationship and institutional benefits without replacing the learner-facing story. Do not transfer unsupported metrics, universal standards acceptance or automatic verification claims.

The revised Skill Passport solution must explain how a learner carries, presents and shares a connected record. It is not another CLR infrastructure page: certificate = one achievement; CLR = connected structured record; Skill Passport = the learner-facing, portable expression of that record.

**Why:** The owner explicitly revised the page's purpose and asked for this three-way distinction to be immediately understandable. Institutional value remains a benefit, not a reason to replace the learner-facing story.

**How to apply:** Lead with the learner's presentation and sharing experience. Keep institution-issued, learner-added and attested evidence distinct, with verification at item level rather than implying all passport content is institution-verified.

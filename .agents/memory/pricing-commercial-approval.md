---
name: Pricing commercial approval
description: Proposed pricing packaging requires commercial sign-off before publication.
---

The pricing redesign brief supplies proposed packaging recommendations, not confirmed commercial commitments. A request to implement the page is not approval to publish those allowances or entitlements.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires the CertifyMe team to approve credential, recipient, administrator, department and project allowances before production publication.

**How to apply:** Keep approval information private. Before publishing pricing changes, obtain explicit commercial approval of capacity limits and conditional feature packaging. Do not infer approval from a successful build, a visual approval, or a request to redesign the page.

The owner specifies: “Essentials should start from 500 onwards students per year, no limit on credentials; Professional should start from 1000 onwards per year; custom domain and email is only for Professional onwards.”

**Why:** These are the owner's commercial corrections to the proposed packaging, not maximum student allowances.

**How to apply:** Preserve annual student starting thresholds rather than reverting to credential-volume caps. Custom domain and email must not be offered in Essentials. These corrections do not approve every remaining proposed allowance or authorize publication.

Skill Passport access is restricted to Professional and Enterprise as an add-on, not Essentials.

**Why:** The owner specifies “access to Skill Passport is only at Enterprise and Professional based as addon.”

**How to apply:** Do not present Skill Passport as a core-plan inclusion or an Essentials add-on.

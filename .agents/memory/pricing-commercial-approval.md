Earlier redesign briefs supplied proposed packaging recommendations, not confirmed commercial commitments. The owner's later master implementation specification explicitly approves its stated rules; do not re-run product strategy or infer missing commercial values.

**Why:** The owner calls the master specification the source of product and commercial decisions, while requiring missing facts to be reported as blockers rather than invented.

**How to apply:** Apply explicit master-spec rules without another packaging exercise. Keep approval information private. Obtain confirmation for unspecified quotas and conditional package assignments before publication; implementation is not authorization to publish.

The owner specifies: “Essentials should start from 500 onwards students per year, no limit on credentials; Professional should start from 1000 onwards per year; custom domain and email is only for Professional onwards.”

**Why:** These are the owner's commercial corrections to the proposed packaging, not maximum student allowances.

**How to apply:** Preserve annual student starting thresholds rather than reverting to credential-volume caps. Custom domain and email must not be offered in Essentials. These corrections do not approve every remaining proposed allowance or authorize publication.

Skill Passport access is restricted to Professional and Enterprise as an add-on, not Essentials.

**Why:** The owner specifies “access to Skill Passport is only at Enterprise and Professional based as addon.”

**How to apply:** Do not present Skill Passport as a core-plan inclusion or an Essentials add-on.

When applying the final corrective pricing brief, the owner explicitly selected “Keep your recent corrections”: apply its other content and UX requirements without reinstating its conflicting commercial caps or branding/module entitlements.

**Why:** The owner resolved the contradiction between the uploaded brief and their direct commercial instructions.

**How to apply:** Treat the recent commercial corrections above as authoritative when reconciling that brief.

The master specification approves Essentials: exactly 1 administrator, exactly 3 templates, zero departments, no department-management/add-ons, standard CertifyMe branding and no custom-branding entitlement. Professional: exactly 10 templates, 1 included department, additional departments as paid recurring add-ons, fine-grained department permissions and supported custom branding. Preserve its approved configured administrator allowance and other quotas; do not use earlier illustrative values as configuration.

**Why:** These are explicit commercial rules in the owner's master implementation specification, not suggested packaging.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing customer entitlements backwards-compatibly. Updating a marketing table is not evidence of runtime enforcement. If application authorization/billing configuration or approved add-on prices are unavailable, report the blocker and do not create substitute enforcement or invent a price.

---
name: Professional association positioning
description: Buyer and category scope for the association credential solution.
---

The association page is “NOT primarily for individual badge recipients.” Lead with digital credential infrastructure for professional associations, membership organizations, certification bodies and continuing-professional-development organizations.

**Why:** The owner explicitly wants executives, membership, certification, education and CPD leaders to see connected achievement infrastructure rather than a generic badge generator.

**How to apply:** Connect member recognition to association-governed certification, learning, reviewed skills and professional records. Member benefits support the institutional buyer story. Do not describe renewal decisions or regulatory acceptance as automatic platform capabilities.

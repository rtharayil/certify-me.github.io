---
name: CertifyMe colour consistency
description: Preserve the established site colour theme during page refreshes.
---

Keep refreshed CertifyMe pages aligned with the existing site colour theme rather than introducing independent page palettes.

**Why:** The owner flagged that the Comprehensive Learner Record and Workforce Intelligence refreshes were moving away from their colour theme.

**How to apply:** Match the current site's colours when revising backgrounds, buttons, diagrams and interactive states. A page refresh is not permission to introduce a new colour theme.

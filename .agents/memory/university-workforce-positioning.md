---
name: University Workforce Intelligence positioning
description: Why this solution must be presented as one university-facing offering instead of a student career marketplace.
---

Workforce Intelligence is a single B2B solution for universities. The buyer and decision-maker is the institution: careers, curriculum, employer engagement, workforce development and leadership teams. Keep the story as credentials → skills → workforce → careers; occupations, employer demand, pathways and job context are layers of the same solution, not separate consumer products. Jobs may illustrate demand but must not become the page's primary CTA or a learner application flow.

**Why:** The user explicitly removed the earlier CareerIQ learner job-board demo and then clarified the university-facing positioning in a replacement brief. The public static site also does not provide an authenticated learner account or live job data.

**How to apply:** On future revisions to the Workforce Intelligence solution, lead with institutional questions and product evidence. Label illustrative mappings and market concepts; do not show fabricated demand numbers, real-employer claims or implied live vacancies without a connected and documented data source.

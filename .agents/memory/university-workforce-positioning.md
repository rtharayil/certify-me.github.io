---
name: University Workforce Intelligence positioning
description: Why this solution must be presented as one university-facing offering instead of a student career marketplace.
---

Workforce Intelligence is a single B2B solution for universities. The buyer and decision-maker is the institution: careers, curriculum, employer engagement, workforce development and leadership teams. Keep the story as programme → learning outcomes → skills → occupations → employer demand → institutional decision. Occupations, employer demand, pathways and job context are layers of the same solution, not separate consumer products. Jobs may inform demand but must not become the page's primary CTA or a learner application flow.

**Why:** The user explicitly removed the earlier CareerIQ learner job-board demo and clarified the university-facing positioning. The revised brief centres three buyer questions: what skills programmes develop, which occupations use them, and how employer demand is changing. The public static site does not provide an authenticated learner account or live job data.

**How to apply:** Lead with institutional questions and product concepts. Visibly distinguish institutional evidence from separately sourced and mapped external market data. Do not show fabricated demand numbers, real-employer demand claims or implied live vacancies without a connected and documented data source. Preserve approved Job Engine claims without implying that the public dashboard performs a live query.

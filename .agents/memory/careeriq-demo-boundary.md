---
name: Public CareerIQ demo boundary
description: Why the Solutions experience cannot be presented as a live career marketplace without a secure integration.
---

The user chose to put the CareerIQ experience in the public CertifyMe Solutions area rather than establish a separate authenticated learner app. Treat the public experience as an interactive example: fictional learners, skills, roles, employers, scores and locally saved drafts must not be described as verified credentials, active vacancies or submitted applications.

**Why:** The existing website is static and public; it has neither authenticated learner records nor a licensed jobs feed or employer submission channel. Presenting its demo as live would mislead learners and could expose private credential or application data if collection were added casually.

**How to apply:** Before moving beyond the demo, establish an authenticated service, authorised sources of credentials and jobs, employer delivery, learner consent controls and secure persistence. Keep public example pages excluded from indexing as real job postings, and do not collect personal application information in browser storage.

The SEO strategy positions CertifyMe as “Digital Credential Infrastructure for Institutions,” not primarily for consumer searches. Higher education is the strongest commercial vertical, alongside certification bodies, enterprise, government, and training providers. Preserve the six-layer journey and existing brand, typography, animations and UX unless an SEO, accessibility, crawlability or conversion change requires otherwise.

The execution priority is an ordering rule, not permission to leave locally fixable defects unfinished. Finish those defects before delivery; distinguish missing production access or business evidence from work simply not completed.

**Why:** The owner challenged the previous delivery's many partial statuses and explicitly requested that the outstanding defects be fixed.

**How to apply:** Do not equate a passing priority gate with completion of the entire brief. Report completed work, remaining evidence-dependent decisions and externally unverified checks separately.

**Why:** The owner's enterprise SEO brief broadens the institutional buyer scope while retaining higher education as the strongest commercial vertical and explicitly excludes unnecessary visual redesign.

**How to apply:** Lead with institutional buyer intent and the higher-education pillar. Reuse existing canonical pages and comparison articles rather than creating duplicate URLs. Before deleting or merging articles, check backlinks, organic traffic, indexing and ranking keywords. Customer relationships, quotes, integrations and outcomes require verified and approved evidence; do not invent missing proof.

For comprehensive SEO work, follow audit → plan → implement → structural test → fix → independent adversarial test → fix → final report. Stop and flag important unverifiable business facts rather than inventing replacements.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires both testing rounds and evidence-based claim handling.

**How to apply:** Do not present a prior narrow audit or a pre-implementation inventory as either required testing round. Keep unknown indexing, traffic, backlink, security, and operational facts explicit.

Comparison pages are CertifyMe marketing, not neutral procurement summaries: aim for approximately 80% useful factual buyer information and 20% positive differentiation. Explain the connected institutional journey with competitor-specific buyer framing. Don't make CertifyMe win by claiming competitors are bad. Make CertifyMe win because the buyer can clearly see a broader, connected architecture, with the Job Engine, skills taxonomy, CLR and workforce layer providing factual evidence that it extends beyond credential issuance.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested a stronger reason to consider CertifyMe through factual connected architecture, not aggressive selling or negative competitor claims.

**How to apply:** Preserve useful vendor facts and capability overlap. Do not infer absence from missing public documentation, claim exclusive superiority or replace distinct comparisons with the same sales opening.

## Content-authority-only work

For the owner's content-authority phase, do not rerun the original 33-part transformation or redo passing technical, positioning and authority-connection work. Complete the full-body inventory before selected P0 edits; determine parents from article meaning, not title keywords. Shared parent/navigation modules are not contextual editorial body links.

**Why:** The owner explicitly said the previous transformation had already been executed twice and requested curated content authority instead of another structural pass.

**How to apply:** Use a new scope-specific content-authority validation, retain baseline scores as baseline, and separate executed changes from deferred recommendations. Future articles need a content-reviewed parent decision; do not reintroduce title matching as a fallback.

name: Institutional SEO scope
description: Owner-directed B2B SEO positioning and safeguards for existing URLs and evidence.
---

The SEO strategy positions CertifyMe as “Digital Credential Infrastructure for Institutions,” not primarily for consumer searches. Higher education is the strongest commercial vertical, alongside certification bodies, enterprise, government, and training providers. Preserve the six-layer journey and existing brand, typography, animations and UX unless an SEO, accessibility, crawlability or conversion change requires otherwise.

**Why:** The owner's enterprise SEO brief broadens the institutional buyer scope while retaining higher education as the strongest commercial vertical and explicitly excludes unnecessary visual redesign.

**How to apply:** Lead with institutional buyer intent and the higher-education pillar. Reuse existing canonical pages and comparison articles rather than creating duplicate URLs. Before deleting or merging articles, check backlinks, organic traffic, indexing and ranking keywords. Customer relationships, quotes, integrations and outcomes require verified and approved evidence; do not invent missing proof.

For comprehensive SEO work, follow audit → plan → implement → structural test → fix → independent adversarial test → fix → final report. Stop and flag important unverifiable business facts rather than inventing replacements.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires both testing rounds and evidence-based claim handling.

**How to apply:** Do not present a prior narrow audit or a pre-implementation inventory as either required testing round. Keep unknown indexing, traffic, backlink, security, and operational facts explicit.

---
name: Public proof boundaries
description: Independent evidence limits for standards, review rankings, and security claims on CertifyMe pages.
---

The public 1EdTech registry lists CertifyMe as an active Open Badges 3.0 **Issuer** (also CLR 2.0 Issuer/Displayer), but its entry should not be used to imply that every credential or interoperability scenario is certified. The public G2 review profile supports a review rating and count at a point in time; it does not establish a current, dated Leader report. A vendor-authored screenshot or article is not enough to establish a current G2 Leader designation, SOC 2 Type II audit, ISO certification, or geographic/issuance totals.

**Why:** Public-facing proof was presented as current despite no independent, dated report or underlying operational evidence for several claims. Certification scopes and review ratings change.

**How to apply:** Link to the 1EdTech registry for exact certification scope; cite a dated G2 report before claiming a Leader award. Require the relevant current audit report/certificate and internally reconciled metric definitions before restoring security or reach claims anywhere on the site.

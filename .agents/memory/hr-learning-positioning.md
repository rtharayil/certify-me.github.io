---
name: Enterprise HR/L&D positioning
description: Standing buyer and product boundaries for the CertifyMe HR/L&D solution.
---

For enterprise HR and L&D messaging, address Chief Learning Officers, L&D directors, talent development, corporate academy and credential leaders before individual employees. Lead with Learning → Achievement → Credential → Verification → Skills → Employee Learning Record. Describe CertifyMe as credential and skill infrastructure around the buyer’s existing learning systems. Workforce intelligence and opportunity are optional downstream context, not the product promise on this page.

**Why:** The owner explicitly replaced the old employee-recognition-led HR page with this learning-first enterprise positioning. A badge generator, HRIS, talent-management platform or job platform is the wrong buyer story.

**How to apply:** Use this hierarchy when revising HR/L&D marketing, selecting media, writing FAQ answers or linking related solutions. Keep claims about skills and verification tied to recognized achievements and supported proof, not automatic competence or independently checked identity.

---
name: Skills taxonomy positioning
description: Strategic boundaries for the institution-facing skills taxonomy solution.
---

Present Skills Taxonomy Mapping as an institutional skills interpretation and interoperability layer for university leaders, technology teams and professional or chartered bodies. Their own learning outcomes, competency language and certification standards remain authoritative. Skills relationships should be described as reviewed and tied to achievement evidence, not as automatic extraction of certain meaning from a badge.

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritised strategic programme value, institutional control, data provenance and professional-body authority over a consumer skills tool. Public references to ESCO and O*NET do not establish a supported native integration or validated institution-specific crosswalk.

**How to apply:** Keep external taxonomies as reference concepts until product capability and mappings can be verified. Distinguish credential claims, learner records and market data sources; do not imply employment outcomes or live demand from skills mapping alone.

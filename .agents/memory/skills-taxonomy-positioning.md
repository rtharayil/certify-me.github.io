---
name: Skills taxonomy positioning
description: Strategic boundaries for the institution-facing skills taxonomy solution.
---

Present Skills Taxonomy Mapping as an institutional skills interpretation and interoperability layer, not a general university digital credential page. Its primary positioning is “Skills Taxonomy Mapping,” without “for Universities.” Institutions' own learning outcomes, competency language and certification standards remain authoritative. Skills relationships should be described as reviewed and tied to achievement evidence, not as automatic extraction of certain meaning from a badge.

**Why:** The user's revised solution brief centres the mapping process and institutional judgement, rather than a university-wide credential story. Public references to ESCO and O*NET do not establish a supported native integration or validated institution-specific crosswalk.

**How to apply:** Explain learning outcome → evidence → institutional review → skill → taxonomy → credential → learner record → workforce context visually. Keep explorable examples illustrative and external taxonomies as reference concepts until product capability and mappings can be verified. Never suggest that AI automatically decides what an achievement means. Distinguish credential claims, learner records and market data sources; do not imply employment outcomes or live demand from skills mapping alone.

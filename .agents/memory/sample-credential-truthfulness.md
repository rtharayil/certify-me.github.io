---
name: Sample credential truthfulness
description: How to represent the homepage credential preview without confusing it with a real issued credential.
---

The homepage interactive credential should be one coherent fictional university specimen, not a real person's linked credential. Its issuer, recipient, certificate artwork, proof details, and role/skill examples must agree. Mark it as a sample rather than an issued, cryptographically verified credential. ESCO/O*NET alignments are illustrative concepts, not certified taxonomy mappings; job intelligence previews must not imply that fictional employers, trend bars, and skill matches are live vacancy data or endorsements. FERPA is illustrative programme context, not an occupational skill or learner certification.

The user wants a guided visual walkthrough of the **features a sample certification could include**, not a live sample verification page. Highlight standards representation, scannable identifiers, ID tagging, email OTP, signed-document and Open Badge verification flows, a learning path, and job intelligence. Autoplay should loop until the visitor interacts, then hand over control; respect reduced motion. File drop in the walkthrough must stay local and must not claim to authenticate the file; sample codes identify the fictional certificate but are not proof of validity. Social sharing should link to this fictional tour, not present the sample as a learner's issued credential. A pathway from the fictional certificate to further learning should be framed as a possible next step, not an actual enrolment, active learner record, or personalised recommendation.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected a real certificate inserted into a generic product demo and clarified that the goal is to show all product features through a visual wizard, not offer a real verifier. Mixing a real person's credential with simulated Open Badges 3.0, taxonomy, and job data made the experience feel pasted together and falsely connected the claims. The linked original offers an OpenBadge 2.1 export, so it cannot substantiate an Open Badges 3.0 verified claim.

**How to apply:** Preserve the coherent fictional university narrative and interactive but transparent demo states when revising the homepage. Verify format and data provenance independently before upgrading any illustrative claims to live or verified.

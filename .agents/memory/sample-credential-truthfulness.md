---
name: Sample credential truthfulness
description: How to represent the homepage credential preview without confusing it with a real issued credential.
---

The homepage interactive credential is illustrative. Do not describe the linked real example as an Open Badges 3.0 verified record: its public page offers an OpenBadge 2.1 JSON export. Job matches in the preview are examples rather than live vacancy data or endorsements, and the FERPA tag is illustrative program context rather than certification.

**Why:** Combining a mock Open Badges 3.0 experience with a real verification link could otherwise imply that the real record and simulated workforce data prove claims they do not.

**How to apply:** Keep clear preview labeling when revising credential marketing or sample interactions. Verify format and data provenance independently before upgrading claims to live or verified.

---
name: Credential portability claims
description: Accuracy boundary for standards, migration, and verification language in CertifyMe comparison content.
---

Do not describe Open Badges or W3C Verifiable Credentials as making verification independent of every platform or vendor. Standards-based proof can be checked outside a single presentation interface, but ongoing verification can still depend on issuer keys and applicable status or revocation resources.

**Why:** Absolute portability claims overstate what credential standards guarantee and create an avoidable factual inconsistency across comparison pages.

**How to apply:** In migration and competitor comparisons, describe open standards as reducing dependency on a single interface or format, and advise buyers to confirm issuer-key, status-resource, export, and continuity arrangements in writing.

---
name: Hero summary length
description: The user's preferred separation between brief hero copy and detailed institutional abstracts.
---

Pages with long abstracts should show only a two- or three-line summary below the hero heading, with the full abstract moved farther down.

**Why:** The user identified the lengthy Comprehensive Learner Record introduction and matching pages as too much text in the opening.

**How to apply:** Keep the opening concise across desktop and mobile widths. Preserve the full explanation in lower-page content rather than truncating or deleting it.

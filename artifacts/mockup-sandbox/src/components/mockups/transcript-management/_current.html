02

The institutional challenge

The transcript is one of the university's most important records. Managing it should not depend on manual workflows.

Requests often touch email, document preparation, printing or scanning, postage, follow-ups and repeated verification. Each hand-off adds work for registrar and student-service teams—and leaves learners asking about status.

Students and alumni need records at consequential moments: graduate admissions, employment, licensing, immigration, international study and credential evaluation.

A transcript request is not an isolated administrative transaction. It is part of the learner's academic and professional journey.

  • Manual requests and email follow-ups
  • Staff intervention and document preparation
  • Printing, scanning and postage
  • Repeated verification and fraud-check enquiries
  • Status questions across fragmented systems

10

Document integrity

Make transcript tampering detectable.

A cryptographic signature provides integrity and issuer-authenticity evidence for the signed document. It does not, by itself, establish every aspect of recipient identity.

ORIGINALInstitution-approved transcript
SIGNCryptographic signature
ISSUESigned document
VERIFYCheck the signature
RESULTCompare integrity
MATCHSignature check matches; document integrity is supported.
MISMATCHIntegrity check failed or document changed; investigate with the issuer.

A mismatch indicates an integrity failure or alteration; it does not automatically establish fraud. Issuer and recipient checks remain distinct.

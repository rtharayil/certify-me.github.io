A transparent way to scope your plan

Pricing that scales with your institution

CertifyMe plans are designed around the scale and complexity of your credential operations.

Your subscription defines the administrative capabilities and organizational scope available to your team. Annual credential volume defines your included issuance capacity, while recipient allowances help establish the population covered by your subscription.

Institutions with higher volumes, more complex organizational structures, or additional integration and governance requirements can discuss a tailored arrangement with our team.

Capacity measure

How many credentials you issue

Your annual allowance covers the number of digital credentials issued during the subscription year.

Covered population

How many learners your subscription covers

A recipient is a person receiving credentials. One recipient may receive multiple credentials.

Operational scope

How your team manages credential operations

Administrative accounts, departments, projects, and permissions determine how your organization manages its credential programs.

Recipients and administrative users are different. A student receiving a credential is not the same as an administrator managing the platform. Discuss additional capacity with our team; capacity and any associated charges are agreed with the customer.

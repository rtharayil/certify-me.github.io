A transparent way to scope your plan

Pricing that scales with your institution

CertifyMe plans are designed around the scale and complexity of your credential operations.

Your subscription defines the students covered per year, administrative capabilities, and organizational scope available to your team. Essentials and Professional have no limit on credentials issued to covered students.

Institutions with higher volumes, more complex organizational structures, or additional integration and governance requirements can discuss a tailored arrangement with our team.

Capacity measure

Annual credential volume

How many credentials you issue during the subscription year. Essentials and Professional have no credential issuance limit for covered students. Enterprise capacity is tailored by agreement.

Covered population

Recipient population

How many learners or recipients your subscription covers. A recipient is a person receiving credentials. One recipient may receive multiple credentials.

Operational scope

Administrative users and organizational scope

How your team manages credential operations across departments, projects, and permissions. Administrative users are team members who access the CertifyMe management platform.

Recipients and administrative users are different. A student receiving a credential is not the same as an administrator managing the platform. Discuss additional capacity with our team; capacity and any associated charges are agreed with the customer.

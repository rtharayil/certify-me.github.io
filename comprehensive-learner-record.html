The wider picture

A Learner's Story Is Bigger Than a Transcript

A transcript remains essential. It does not always capture every micro-credential, project, placement, professional milestone or skill developed along the way. These achievements often live in different systems; a CLR offers a structured way to bring relevant parts of the journey together.

An essential academic record

Transcript

Documents courses, grades and formal academic progression.

A broader learning record

A CLR complements the transcript. It does not replace the authoritative academic record.

Provenance & verification

A Learner Record Should Be More Than a Static Document

A verifiable record depends on who issued an achievement, what was claimed and how its status can be checked. The 1EdTech CLR Standard describes a format for interoperable learner records; W3C Verifiable Credentials provide a model for machine-verifiable claims.

Across organisations

Learning Doesn't Come From One Institution

A learner's record may connect achievements from a university, professional body, training provider and employer. Each organisation remains the source of its own claims; bringing achievements together does not transfer or guarantee their validity.

Standards & governance

Standards First. Institutionally Governed.

1EdTech's CLR 2.0 standard, Open Badges and the W3C Verifiable Credentials model provide relevant technical foundations. An institution still decides what an achievement means, what evidence supports it and who can issue it. A reference to a standard is not a claim that this example record is certified or independently verified.

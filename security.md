# Feature 1 — ISO 27001
Feature1Image: /images/iso_27001-1.png
Feature1Title: ISO 27001 Certified — Information Security Management
Feature1Text: CertifyMe holds ISO 27001 certification, the internationally recognised standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). This certification confirms that CertifyMe has implemented and maintains controls covering risk assessment, access control, incident management, business continuity, and supplier security — independently verified by an accredited third-party auditor. Institutions issuing credentials through CertifyMe can rely on a platform built to enterprise security standards.

# Feature 2 — ISO 27701 + ISO 9001
Feature2Image: /images/iso-27701.png
Feature2Title: ISO 27701 Certified — Privacy Information Management
Feature2Text: ISO 27701 extends ISO 27001 to cover the management of personally identifiable information (PII). CertifyMe's ISO 27701 certification demonstrates that privacy controls are embedded across the organisation's systems and processes — not treated as an afterthought. Paired with ISO 9001 (Quality Management System) certification, CertifyMe delivers a platform where security, privacy, and operational quality are continuously audited and improved.

# Feature 3 — GDPR, FERPA, COPPA
Feature3Image: /images/gdpr-image.png
Feature3Title: GDPR, FERPA & COPPA Compliant — Global Data Privacy Coverage
Feature3Text: CertifyMe is compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for European users, the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) for US student educational records, and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). A formal GDPR compliance document is available for review. No sensitive learner data — including email addresses — is required for credential issuance, minimising data exposure by design.

# Feature 4 — Credential-level security
Feature4Title: Tamper-Proof Credentials with Cryptographic Signatures & QR Verification
Feature4Text: Every credential issued through CertifyMe is protected with a cryptographic digital signature and a unique QR code or verification URL. Recipients and verifiers can independently confirm the authenticity of any credential without contacting CertifyMe — the verification is self-contained. Credentials are stored with AES-grade encryption, and the platform supports blockchain-backed tamper-proofing for institutions requiring the highest level of credential integrity.

Q4Title: How are credentials protected against tampering or fraud?

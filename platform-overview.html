Digital Credential Infrastructure for Institutions

Digital credential infrastructure connects issuance, standards, verification and record governance. CertifyMe helps institutional teams organise these capabilities around trusted achievements rather than a badge image alone.

Explore digital credentials for universities, Open Badges 3.0 and credential verification, then connect the evidence through skills taxonomy mapping, Comprehensive Learner Records and workforce intelligence.

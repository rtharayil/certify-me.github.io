  <h2>Institutional evaluation checklist</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Academic records:</strong> distinguish degrees, transcripts, certificates, digital badges and microcredentials.</li>
    <li><strong>Standards:</strong> check the exact Open Badges 3.0 and CLR certification roles, W3C credential formats, and receiving-system compatibility.</li>
    <li><strong>Trust:</strong> review proof validation, issuer keys, status or revocation resources, verification access and continuity arrangements.</li>
    <li><strong>Records and skills:</strong> assess Comprehensive Learner Records, reviewed skills mappings and the provenance of learner evidence.</li>
    <li><strong>Implementation:</strong> confirm SIS, LMS and API workflows, support ownership, security evidence and data residency.</li>
    <li><strong>Experience and analytics:</strong> distinguish sharing and engagement metrics from measured educational or workforce outcomes.</li>
    <li><strong>Workforce capabilities:</strong> request the labour-market source, licensing, geography, coverage and refresh dates.</li>
  </ul>

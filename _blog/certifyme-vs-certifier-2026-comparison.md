Certifier and CertifyMe both offer digital achievement credentialing. Treat this as an institutional evaluation, not a claim that one only makes PDFs. This comparison is published by CertifyMe, and sources were checked on 4 October 2026.

## Certifier is more than a PDF generator

[Certifier's current site](https://certifier.io/) describes an AI-powered platform for **verifiable certificates, credentials and badges**, connecting to existing tools and running certifications from one place. Its [integration directory](https://certifier.io/integrations) is an appropriate starting point for a connector checklist. Those descriptions do not justify labeling the product event-only or asserting that a university will outgrow it.

For a continuing-education team, evaluate recipient delivery, the distinction between a downloaded PDF and its verifiable record, renewal/correction handling and integration entitlement. Ask for exact standards versions, proof mechanisms and current certification identifiers. Do not infer lack of support from a homepage that does not mention a feature.

## What to test with Certifier

Match annual issuance limits and billing to your programme. Test an OB3 export, the actual CRM/LMS workflow, roles, audit logs and SSO. Do not equate ISO certification with a SOC 2 report.

Use the same authorized test achievement on each shortlisted platform. Inspect issuer, recipient reference, criteria/evidence, exported format and proof. Test the receiving system, status changes, corrections and unavailable verification resources. Measure issuance and engagement separately from skills attainment or employment outcomes.

## Commercial and security due diligence

Compare volume units, billing/renewal terms, entitlement, implementation and support, not isolated list prices. Review current security reports/certificates and their period, product scope and customer controls. Specify storage, backups, subprocessors, support access and international transfers in the contract.

## Compare the actual certification workflow

| Requirement | Evidence available | Question for both vendors |
|---|---|---|
| Design and issuance | Both publish credential creation offerings | Can our authorized staff issue an accessible, correct award? |
| Verifiable record | Certifier describes verifiable credentials; CertifyMe has listed issuer roles | What is exported and what proof can our receiver check? |
| Lifecycle | Current behavior must be demonstrated | How do correction, renewal, expiry and status changes behave? |
| Programme integration | Both publish integration offerings | Which exact connector and plan handles our completion event? |
| Skills and records | CertifyMe describes institutional skills/record scope | How are outcome mappings reviewed and provenance retained? |

A strong pilot includes a duplicate completion event, an incorrect recipient address, an amended award and an unavailable status service. Record what needs manual intervention. A fast design experience does not by itself establish assessment quality, and a cryptographic proof does not independently identify the learner.

faqs:
  - question: "Is a digital credential just a PDF of my certificate?"
    answer: "Not even close. A PDF is just a picture of paper. A real digital credential has metadata baked into it that proves who issued it and that it hasn't been messed with. It’s the difference between a photocopy of a passport and the real thing."
  - question: "Why are schools switching away from paper?"
    answer: "Paper is a nightmare to manage. It gets lost, it's expensive to mail, and it's way too easy to fake. Digital credentials are fast, secure, and—let's be honest—much cooler for students to share on social media."
  - question: "Can I use these credentials to get a job?"
    answer: "In my experience, yes. More and more recruiters are looking for verified proof. If you can send them a link that instantly validates your skills, you're already ahead of the person sending a generic resume."
  - question: "Do I have to pay to keep my digital credentials?"
    answer: "Usually, no. Once a school or company issues them to you, they're yours to keep in your digital wallet. At CertifyMe, we believe you should own your achievements without a subscription fee."

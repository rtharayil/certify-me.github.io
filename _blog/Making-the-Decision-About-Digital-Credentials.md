The right digital credential approach depends on what an institution needs its awards to do. A certificate maker produces a visual document. Connected credential infrastructure carries the achievement into issuance, verification, learner records and the skills context that makes it useful beyond the programme.

## Beyond certificate design

An institutional award has an issuer, approved criteria, a recipient and an ongoing record. The [full credential lifecycle](/platform-overview) connects those elements from approval through issuance and maintenance. CertifyMe brings credential presentation into that broader operating context.

## Structured achievement data and verification

The [W3C credential model](/blog/Understanding-W3C-Verifiable-Credentials.html) describes issuer, holder and verifier relationships. [Open Badges 3.0](/blog/why-institutions-should-embrace-open-badges-3-0-standards) adds structured achievement information. Compatible receiving systems can interpret the record and check its supported proof and status.

<img class="img-fluid r-16" src="/img/blog/mistakes-to-avoid.png" alt="Challenges of digital transformation" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;">

## Individual awards within a broader learner record

A [Comprehensive Learner Record](/comprehensive-learner-record) connects achievements with their issuers and evidence, complementing the transcript and student information system. It helps institutions represent learning that extends across programmes and award types.

<img class="img-fluid r-16" src="/img/blog/digital-credential-myth.png" alt="Myths of digital credentials" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;">

## Academic meaning connected to career relevance

Academic teams govern assessed outcomes, and registrars retain authority over official records. [Workforce information](/workforce-intelligence) adds occupation and employer-demand context to reviewed skills, giving career services another resource for learner guidance.

## A workflow that fits institutional operations

Approval rules, recipient access, corrections and record continuity shape how a credential programme operates. CertifyMe's issuance, verification and connected-record capabilities provide a foundation around which those institutional workflows can be configured.

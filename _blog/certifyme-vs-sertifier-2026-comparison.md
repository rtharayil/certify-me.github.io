<p data-comparison-summary="sertifier">Sertifier documents enterprise credential workflows, Open Badges, W3C VCs, API/webhooks, configurable EU/US residency and a dedicated CLR product. These are shared capabilities to evaluate, not grounds for declaring CertifyMe uniquely broader. CertifyMe describes institution-governed infrastructure connecting credentials, verification, reviewed skills, learner records and workforce context. These capabilities overlap: choose against your programme's records, receiving systems, evidence and contract rather than assuming a universal winner.</p>

## What to test with Sertifier

Test the exact Open Badges version, VC export/proof and CLR profile rather than relying on broad standards wording. Confirm residency across backups/subprocessors and obtain the applicable security assurance and enterprise SLA.

Use the same authorized test achievement on each shortlisted platform. Inspect issuer, recipient reference, criteria/evidence, exported format and proof. Test the receiving system, status changes, corrections and unavailable verification resources. Measure issuance and engagement separately from skills attainment or employment outcomes.

## Commercial and security due diligence

Compare volume units, billing/renewal terms, entitlement, implementation and support, not isolated list prices. Review current security reports/certificates and their period, product scope and customer controls. Specify storage, backups, subprocessors, support access and international transfers in the contract.

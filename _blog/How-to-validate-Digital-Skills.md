faqs:
  - question: "Why isn't just knowing the software enough?"
    answer: "In a stack of resumes, everyone says they know Excel. Having a verified badge or certificate proves you actually put in the effort to learn it properly. It gives employers that extra bit of confidence to pick you over someone else."
  - question: "Can university staff benefit from these too?"
    answer: "Definitely. Earning badges for tools like Salesforce or data dashboards shows you're keeping up with best practices. It's not just for students; it's a great way for all of us to show we're growing in our roles."
  - question: "Is it hard to keep track of multiple digital badges?"
    answer: "Actually, it's pretty easy if you use a digital wallet or embed them in your LinkedIn profile. They all live in one place, so you don't have to worry about losing them like you would with old paper certificates."
  - question: "How do I choose which skills to validate first?"
    answer: "I'd start with the tools you use every day—maybe it's Zoom, Trello, or Google Workspace. Validating those 'micro-skills' can have a huge impact on how professional your digital presence looks to others."

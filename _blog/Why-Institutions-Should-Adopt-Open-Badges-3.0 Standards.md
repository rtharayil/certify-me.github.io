## Start with the achievement model

Define the issuer, achievement, criteria and supporting evidence. State whether an award recognizes participation, completion or demonstrated competence. Programme governance supplies the meaning; a standard does not independently perform assessment.

## Understand the relationship to W3C credentials

The [W3C Verifiable Credentials model](/blog/Understanding-W3C-Verifiable-Credentials.html) distinguishes issuer, holder and verifier roles. Check the supported securing mechanism and resources needed for proof and status verification; do not assume every implementation uses the same mechanism.

## Evaluate issuer certification accurately

The [1EdTech registry](https://site.imsglobal.org/certifications/certifyme/certifyme) is the public source for CertifyMe's OB3 issuer certification scope. Issuer certification is not certification of every integration, receiver, skills relationship or security management system.

## Use a programme-to-record architecture

Place award approval and maintenance within the [institutional credential lifecycle](/platform-overview). Keep assessment evidence attributable, review [achievement-to-skill relationships](/skills-taxonomy-mapping), and preserve provenance when achievements contribute to a [Comprehensive Learner Record](/comprehensive-learner-record).

## Run a compatibility pilot

Issue a fictional or authorized test achievement; inspect the data/proof; test a supported recipient and verifier; exercise an invalid proof or unavailable resource; and record export/continuity requirements. Do not turn a successful one-system demonstration into universal interoperability.

## Review privacy and portability limits

An exported achievement may remain dependent on issuer keys and status resources. Control access to sensitive evidence, and distinguish a signed subject association from independent identity verification.

## Use case evidence with its actual scope

[Anonymous institutional cases](/case-studies.html) demonstrate programme-specific implementation outcomes. They do not establish that OB3 itself guarantees employment, a cost-saving percentage or a particular receiving-system integration.

## Plan your next step

Discuss the actual award, evidence and implementation requirements with your institution. [Request an institutional demo](https://info.certifyme.online/request-demo).

faqs:
  - question: "Can I actually show these badges on my company's LinkedIn page?"
    answer: "You definitely can. I've seen it work best when employees add them to their 'Licenses & Certifications' section, which then links back to your company as the issuer. It's a great way to build some organic brand trust."
  - question: "Do I need a designer to make these look professional?"
    answer: "Not necessarily. While a custom design is great, most platforms give you templates to start with. The key is just making sure the logo is crisp and the colors match your brand identity."
  - question: "Will people actually click on these badges?"
    answer: "If they're curious about someone's skills, yes. A digital badge isn't just a static image; it's a link to a verification page that proves the person actually did the work. That's where the real value is."
  - question: "How do I choose the right platform for my company?"
    answer: "I always tell people to look for three things: ease of use for your team, how good it looks for the recipient, and if it integrates with the tools you're already using. Don't overcomplicate it at the start."

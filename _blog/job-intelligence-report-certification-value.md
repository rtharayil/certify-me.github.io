Job-intelligence reports relate reviewed skill evidence to occupations and separately sourced market information. They support investigation and institutional decisions, not a guarantee of a vacancy match, salary or employment.

## Approved coverage and refresh

The owner confirms 20 million live jobs from 50,000 companies worldwide, refreshed every two weeks, using CertifyMe's own job-farming engine. This is not a claim of real-time refresh, a particular country count, source licensing or salary-field availability.

## Use an evidence–mapping–market chain

Start with an assessed achievement, review the skill relationship, relate it to an occupation and then inspect the separate market source. [Skills taxonomy mapping](/skills-taxonomy-mapping) keeps academic evidence distinct from occupational vocabulary.

## Keep learner records attributable

A [Comprehensive Learner Record](/comprehensive-learner-record) connects achievements with provenance. It does not independently validate the original assessment or turn a market signal into a learner capability.

## Interpret the report rather than promising a result

The [workforce-intelligence authority page](/workforce-intelligence) explains institutional use of occupations and employer demand. Confirm source date, refresh, geography, deduplication and the meaning of any ranking before using a report in curriculum or career decisions.

## Illustrative example: programme relevance review

An academic team could compare a reviewed project-related skill with occupational descriptions and recent employer requirements. The result may suggest an assessment or curriculum question. It does not prove that the learner is ready for every matching role or that a particular vacancy remains open.

## Design the institutional workflow

Within the [credential lifecycle](/platform-overview), assign academic approval, data review and learner-support responsibilities. Career services can help investigate gaps and suitable next steps; programme leaders should not infer placement from report views.

## What evidence can support a claim?

Use the [anonymous case evidence](/case-studies.html) for approved adoption and career-service engagement examples. Keep these measures distinct from hiring outcomes. Confirm raw data source permissions, licensing and contract scope before making stronger data claims.

## Plan your next step

Discuss the actual award, evidence and implementation requirements with your institution. [Request an institutional demo](https://info.certifyme.online/request-demo).

## The infrastructure connecting learning evidence to job intelligence

The report is one output of CertifyMe's Job Engine, not an isolated career-readiness claim. Reviewed learning evidence supplies the skill context; structured job records supply the employer and occupation context. Keep those two sources distinct when academic and career-services teams interpret a report.

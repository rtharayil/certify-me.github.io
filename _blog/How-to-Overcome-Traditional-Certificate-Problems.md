  - question: "Why should we ditch paper certificates for digital ones?"
    answer: "Honestly, paper is just a headache to manage. It's easy to lose, expensive to mail, and a nightmare to verify. Moving to digital saves your staff so much time and makes life easier for your students too."
  - question: "Are digital certificates really safer than paper?"
    answer: "Yes, because they're way harder to fake. With blockchain or cryptographic signatures, you can't just 'edit' a PDF and get away with it. It gives everyone—especially employers—much more confidence that the record is legit."
  - question: "Is it expensive to switch to a digital system?"
    answer: "You'd be surprised at how much you save on printing and postage alone. When you factor in the hours your staff spends on manual verification, the digital route usually pays for itself pretty quickly. It's a much smarter investment."
  - question: "Can students still print their digital certificates if they want to?"
    answer: "Of course! Most platforms let them download a high-quality PDF for framing. They get the best of both worlds—a secure digital record they can share online, and a physical copy for their wall at home."

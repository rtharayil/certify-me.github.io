faqs:
  - question: "What exactly is a digital badge?"
    answer: "Think of it as a 'smart' version of a traditional certificate. It's an image file that's actually embedded with data—proving what you learned, who says you learned it, and when you earned it."
  - question: "How do these badges help my career?"
    answer: "They make your skills visible in a way a resume bullet point can't. Because they're verifiable, an employer can click on your badge and see the actual criteria you met, which builds a lot of trust."
  - question: "Are digital badges only for tech skills?"
    answer: "Not at all. I've seen them used for everything from leadership and soft skills to volunteer work. If there's a skill involved, you can probably badge it."
  - question: "Can I share these badges on social media?"
    answer: "Definitely. Most people put them on LinkedIn, but you can also add them to your email signature or personal website. They're designed to be portable and easy to show off."

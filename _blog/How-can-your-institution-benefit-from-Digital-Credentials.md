Digital credentials help institutions make achievements easier to issue, understand and share. With CertifyMe, the award becomes part of a connected infrastructure: approved achievement data supports issuance and verification, skills provide context, and learner records bring progress together.

## More consistent credential operations

The [institutional credential lifecycle](/platform-overview) connects approval, issuance, delivery, verification and maintenance. This gives credential teams a consistent foundation for managing awards across programmes, including corrections and applicable expiry or revocation.

## Clearer recognition of assessed learning

Academic teams retain control of learning outcomes and assessment criteria. [Reviewed curriculum-to-skill relationships](/skills-taxonomy-mapping) make assessed achievements easier to interpret in recognised capability terms while preserving their original academic meaning.

<img class="img-fluid r-16" src="/img/blog/benefits-of-digital-credentials-for-institutions.png" alt="Benefits of digital credentials for institutions" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;">

## A connected view of learner progress

A [Comprehensive Learner Record](/comprehensive-learner-record) brings achievements and supporting evidence together with issuer provenance. Learners gain a broader presentation of their progress, complementing the university's authoritative transcript and administrative record.

<img class="img-fluid r-16" src="/img/blog/embracing-digital-credentials.png" alt="Ways of embracing digital credentials" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;">

## Structured records built on open standards

[Open Badges 3.0](/blog/why-institutions-should-embrace-open-badges-3-0-standards) represents achievement information in a structured credential. CertifyMe's certified issuer role supports standards-based issuance, while API and integration options connect credential workflows with compatible institutional systems.

## More useful evidence for career conversations

Learners can present assessed achievements when explaining their capabilities. [Workforce intelligence](/workforce-intelligence) adds occupational and employer-demand context, helping career services connect that learning evidence with opportunity research. Engagement, applications and graduate employment remain distinct measures.

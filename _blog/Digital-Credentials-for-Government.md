Government agencies can issue digital records for staff training, professional development and public learning programmes. Those achievement records are different from a national identity document, a benefits authorization or an identity-proofing service. The agency must define the legal and operational purpose before choosing a format.

## Start with the record's authority

For an employee-training badge, identify the agency or approved provider authorized to award it, the completion criteria and the owner of corrections. For a citizen identity or entitlement service, the applicable law, identity assurance and access policy require a separate evaluation. This article does not assert a White House policy using Social Security numbers for digital credential transactions.

The [NIST Digital Identity Guidelines](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/) distinguish identity proofing, authentication and federation. They are a better starting point for an identity-service assessment than treating a signed education credential as proof that a person has been independently identified. The [W3C Verifiable Credentials model](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) describes structured issuer claims and proof relationships; it does not replace those assurance decisions.

## Government credential programme checklist

1. **Authority and eligibility:** document who approves an award and what evidence is required. Distinguish attendance from assessed capability.
2. **Data minimization:** exclude Social Security numbers, case files and unnecessary personal data from public records. Define access, retention and deletion responsibilities.
3. **Accessible delivery:** provide a supported alternative for people without a smartphone or wallet. Test keyboard access, readable pages and low-bandwidth delivery.
4. **Receiver compatibility:** test the actual receiving agency or employer, including the credential format, proof, issuer authority and applicable dates/status.
5. **Lifecycle ownership:** agree who corrects an error, handles a lost link, maintains issuer keys and status resources, and responds when a verification service is unavailable.
6. **Procurement evidence:** request current security documentation, supported hosting regions, access controls and export arrangements. Neither a QR code nor standards conformance establishes these by itself.

## An agency-training example

An agency runs a records-management course. Its training office approves completion, issues a record with course criteria and dates, and retains assessment evidence in an authorized system. Another department can check the record and decide whether the course meets its requirement. It still applies its own staff identity and access checks.

Use the [credential infrastructure overview](/platform-overview) for award governance, and the [Open Badges institutional guide](/blog/why-institutions-should-embrace-open-badges-3-0-standards) for achievement structure. Where multiple awards are connected, a [Comprehensive Learner Record](/comprehensive-learner-record) can retain their provenance; it does not grant an identity entitlement or permission to practise.

## Measure the administrative process

A pilot should compare approval-to-issuance time, support requests, correction rates and successful receiver checks against a documented baseline. These are programme measures, not a promise of fraud elimination or nationwide adoption.

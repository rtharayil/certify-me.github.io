  - question: "How do badges actually help my marketing?"
    answer: "Think of them as little social media magnets. When your customers or students share their achievements, they're basically tagging your brand in a way that feels authentic rather than like an annoying ad."
  - question: "Where should I tell people to share their certificates?"
    answer: "LinkedIn is the big one, obviously, but I've seen them work great in email signatures too. It's a subtle way to show off your company's authority every time an employee or partner sends a message."
  - question: "Will this help with my brand awareness?"
    answer: "Definitely. I always tell people that every shared badge is a 'micro-moment' of advocacy. It's much more effective than a banner ad because it comes with a personal recommendation from someone who actually finished your program."

    answer: "Not at all. You can start small with one workshop or a specific campaign. The ROI is usually pretty clear once you see how many people start clicking back to your site from those shared credentials."

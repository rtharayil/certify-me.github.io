Credly and CertifyMe both serve achievement-credential programmes whose records may be shared outside the issuing organization. Credly should not be portrayed as limited to internal Pearson workforce tracking. This is CertifyMe's own comparison, based on primary pages checked on 4 October 2026—not an independent product ranking.

## Credly documents external discovery and labour-market insights

[Credly's product site](https://info.credly.com/) describes a searchable credential network for training, assessment, learning and certification providers and employers. Its [Occupation Insights page](https://info.credly.com/product/labor-market) describes job postings and labour-market data sourced from global job boards, corporate career sites, the US Census Bureau and Bureau of Labor Statistics. It states that postings are updated 24/7 to display active listings.

This is a vendor-published description, not a new audit of data quality or coverage. It is sufficient to reject the claim that Credly has no external career-market layer. Ask how badge skills are mapped to occupations and how relevant results are filtered for your programme and location.

## What to test with Credly

Distinguish external badge import/verification from your own issuance profile. Test the contracted API, skills data feed, earner experience and integrations. Obtain the exact security and geographic processing terms.

Use the same authorized test achievement on each shortlisted platform. Inspect issuer, recipient reference, criteria/evidence, exported format and proof. Test the receiving system, status changes, corrections and unavailable verification resources. Measure issuance and engagement separately from skills attainment or employment outcomes.

## Commercial and security due diligence

Compare volume units, billing/renewal terms, entitlement, implementation and support, not isolated list prices. Review current security reports/certificates and their period, product scope and customer controls. Specify storage, backups, subprocessors, support access and international transfers in the contract.

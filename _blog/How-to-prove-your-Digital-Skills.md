To prove a digital skill, connect a specific capability to observable work, assessment and credible provenance. A list of software names or a certificate image is not sufficient evidence of competence.

## Use a capability–artifact–assessment pattern

Name the capability in concrete terms, show an appropriate artifact and explain how it was assessed. For collaboration, a project history may be relevant; for data analysis, a documented dataset transformation and interpretation may be more useful than a tool logo.

## Keep the evidence proportionate

Use an authorized portfolio, protected assessment reference or concise description rather than publishing confidential work. Explain what you contributed and what the assessment demonstrates. Do not imply that completing a course proves every related job skill.

## For institutions: review the mapping

Academic teams should review the relationship between assessed outcomes and taxonomy terms. [Skills taxonomy mapping](/skills-taxonomy-mapping) keeps institutional terminology authoritative while relating it to reference vocabularies such as ESCO or O*NET.

## Use credentials to carry the assessed claim

An [Open Badges achievement record](/blog/why-institutions-should-embrace-open-badges-3-0-standards) can describe issuer, criteria and evidence when implemented appropriately. Check what was awarded and whether proof/status resources can be inspected.

<img class="img-fluid r-16" src="/img/blog/upskilling-university-staff-globally.png" alt="Benefits of upskilling university staff digitally" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;">

## Build a record without overstating it

A [Comprehensive Learner Record](/comprehensive-learner-record) can connect achievements and their provenance. It is a way to organize evidence, not an independent identity or competency examination.

## Relate skills to opportunities carefully

Use [workforce intelligence](/workforce-intelligence) to investigate occupational requirements and market signals. A matched term is a prompt for review, not proof of job readiness or a promise of employment. Ask career services to help explain gaps and next learning steps.

## Illustrative portfolio entry

A learner could present an assessed spreadsheet model, a short explanation of data quality decisions and an institution-issued achievement with criteria. The example is fictional and does not imply endorsement by any software vendor.

============================================================

============================================================
CERTIFYME SIX-LAYER MODEL
============================================================

Use the following six-layer model consistently.

Do not rename the layers without a strong reason.

------------------------------------------------------------
01 — PRESENTATION & ACCESS
------------------------------------------------------------

Make achievement visible, branded, accessible and shareable through:

- credential design
- unique credential URLs
- QR access
- credential pages
- sharing
- portable access

Position this as the visible/access layer of the credential.

------------------------------------------------------------
02 — STANDARDS & INTEROPERABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Use recognized standards and interoperable credential models, including where supported:

- Open Badges 3.0
- W3C Verifiable Credentials
- standards-based credential exchange/interoperability

Do not overstate certification.

Clearly distinguish:
- support
- implementation
- certification
- interoperability

Only make the claim supported by current evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------
03 — VERIFICATION & TRUST
------------------------------------------------------------

Establish:

- issuer
- recipient
- achievement
- authenticity
- integrity
- verification
- credential status

using appropriate cryptographic and verification mechanisms.

QR codes are access mechanisms.

Do not describe a QR code itself as proof of authenticity.

------------------------------------------------------------
04 — SKILL TAXONOMY ALIGNMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

Connect achievements to structured skills using, where supported:

- ESCO
- O*NET
- institution-defined/custom frameworks
- competency frameworks

The important idea is:

Achievement
→ evidence
→ learning outcome
→ skill
→ credential

Do not imply that taxonomy mapping automatically proves competence.

Make clear that institutions govern the relationship between learning outcomes, evidence and skills where applicable.

------------------------------------------------------------
05 — COMPREHENSIVE LEARNER RECORD
------------------------------------------------------------

Connect multiple forms of achievement into a broader learner record.

This may include:

- credentials
- degrees
- skills
- competencies
- assessments
- projects
- experiences
- other verified achievements

Position CLR as a connected learner record, not simply another certificate.

Do not imply it automatically replaces the SIS or official transcript.

------------------------------------------------------------
06 — WORKFORCE INTELLIGENCE & OPPORTUNITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Connect verified learner/skill information with workforce intelligence.

This includes:

- skills
- occupations
- jobs
- employers
- labour-market information
- opportunity intelligence
- job intelligence

CertifyMe's Job Engine is a core part of this layer.

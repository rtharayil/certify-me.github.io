## Design Direction

The page should feel like a **world-class enterprise technology partner portal**, not an advertising poster.

Use:

* Premium white and near-black sections
* Extremely clean Swiss-style grid
* Generous whitespace
* Sophisticated typography
* Minimal grey dividers
* High-resolution logo presentation
* Sharp vector-quality assets
* Elegant download buttons
* Consistent card design
* Modern enterprise SaaS aesthetic
* Professional enough for universities, governments, and global technology partners

The page should visually communicate:

**Official · Trusted · Professional · Ready to Use**

The most important element is the **CertifyMe logo itself**. Keep every logo representation extremely sharp, clean, undistorted, and faithful to the supplied official artwork.

Do not create fake partner logos or additional brand marks.

The final output should look like the **official CertifyMe Partner Brand Assets / Logo Download Center**.

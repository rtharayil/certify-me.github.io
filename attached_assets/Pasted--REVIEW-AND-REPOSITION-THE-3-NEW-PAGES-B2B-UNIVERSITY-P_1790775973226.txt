# REVIEW AND REPOSITION THE 3 NEW PAGES — B2B UNIVERSITY PRODUCT

You are reviewing the **three pages that already exist in this project**.

**Do NOT rebuild the website from scratch.**

First inspect the existing routes, components, copy, layouts, graphics and interactions. Then improve the existing implementation.

## CRITICAL BUSINESS POSITIONING

We are a **B2B university technology platform**.

Our customers are universities, colleges and education organisations.

We are NOT currently positioning the product as a B2C student career platform.

Therefore, the `/career-opportunities` page must be repositioned.

The current messaging makes it feel like a student-facing job marketplace where an individual logs in, searches jobs and applies.

**That is NOT the primary product positioning.**

The product provides universities with:

* Career intelligence
* Workforce intelligence
* Labour-market intelligence
* Skills intelligence
* Employer intelligence
* Programme-to-career insights
* Credential-to-skill intelligence
* Career pathway intelligence
* Job-market visibility
* Standards-based credential infrastructure

Job opportunities and applications may exist as part of the product capability, but they should not make the product feel B2C.

# JOB APPLICATION FUNCTIONALITY

Job application functionality can still exist.

However, it should be presented as a **supporting capability**, not the core B2C proposition.

For example:

> Help universities connect verified learner skills with real employment opportunities.

The product may allow learners to:

* View relevant opportunities
* See why their verified skills match
* Identify missing skills
* Share verified credentials
* Apply
* Track applications

But the page should frame this as the **activation layer of the university's career intelligence**, rather than a standalone consumer job platform.

The university should remain the buyer and central user of the story.

---

# IMPORTANT MESSAGE CHANGE

Avoid messaging like:

> Find your next job.

> Jobs matched to your skills.

> Apply directly.

> Your career starts here.

> Search thousands of jobs.

Those phrases create a B2C job-board impression.

Instead use B2B language such as:

> Connect verified skills to real labour-market demand.

> Understand where your learners' skills lead.

> Turn credential data into career intelligence.

> See the occupations, employers and opportunities connected to your programmes.

> Give careers and academic teams a clearer view of learner-to-workforce pathways.

> Move from credentials to skills, and from skills to opportunity.

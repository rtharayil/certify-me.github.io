## 3. Required customer-facing wording

Use the following wording, or concise equivalents that preserve the exact meaning.

### Data encryption in transit and at rest
**All plans:** Encryption of data in transit and at rest is included.

This must be explicitly visible in the comparison table for all three plans. Do not mark it as Enterprise-only or imply that Essentials and Professional lack encryption.

### Credential data security controls
- **Essentials:** Standard credential data security controls.
- **Professional:** Standard credential data security controls.
- **Enterprise:** Custom credential data governance controls, as agreed.

Do not describe standard controls as an absence of security. Enterprise provides agreed customization of governance controls, not an automatic guarantee of unrestricted customization.

### Single sign-on (SSO)
- **Essentials:** Not included.
- **Professional:** Not included.
- **Enterprise:** SSO available.

Do not imply that SSO is available in Essentials or Professional through an unapproved add-on.

### Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- **Essentials:** Not included.
- **Professional:** MFA included.
- **Enterprise:** MFA included.

Keep MFA distinct from SSO. Do not combine them into a single row that obscures the plan differences.

### Backup arrangements
- **Essentials:** No plan-specific backup commitment.
- **Professional:** No plan-specific backup commitment.
- **Enterprise:** Backup arrangements defined by the agreed plan and contract.

Do not imply that the underlying platform does not perform backups where applicable. This comparison concerns the plan-specific backup arrangement or commitment. If existing contractual or technical documentation establishes a baseline service applicable to all plans, do not contradict it; flag the conflict for clarification before publishing.

### Recovery arrangements
- **Essentials:** No plan-specific recovery commitment.
- **Professional:** No plan-specific recovery commitment.
- **Enterprise:** Recovery arrangements defined by the agreed plan and contract.

Do not invent recovery time objectives (RTOs), recovery point objectives (RPOs), or guaranteed recovery times.

### Administrative audit trails
- **Essentials:** Not included as a plan-specific audit-trail feature.
- **Professional:** Basic administrative audit trails.
- **Enterprise:** Advanced administrative audit trails.

Keep basic and advanced auditability distinct. Do not imply that Professional includes every advanced Enterprise audit capability.

### Vulnerability management evidence
- **Essentials:** Not included.
- **Professional:** Not included.
- **Enterprise:** Vulnerability management evidence available.

Do not claim that vulnerability management itself occurs only for Enterprise unless that is independently established. The plan distinction here is access to vulnerability-management evidence.

### Penetration-testing documentation
- **Essentials:** Not included.
- **Professional:** Not included.
- **Enterprise:** Penetration-testing documentation available.

Do not imply that a penetration test has been performed within a particular period, or that a report will always be available for unrestricted distribution, unless confirmed by approved security documentation.

### Security questionnaires and procurement support
- **Essentials:** Not included.
- **Professional:** Not included.
- **Enterprise:** Security questionnaire and procurement support available.

Do not promise completion times or acceptance by a particular procurement authority.

### DPA and contractual privacy terms
- **Essentials:** Standard Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and standard contractual privacy terms.
- **Professional:** Standard Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and standard contractual privacy terms.
- **Enterprise:** Customizable DPA and contractual privacy terms, subject to agreement.

Do not imply that standard privacy obligations are absent from Essentials or Professional. Enterprise allows agreed contractual customization, subject to legal and commercial approval.

### Data residency
- **Essentials:** Data hosted in the APAC region.
- **Professional:** Hosting options in APAC, EU, or US.
- **Enterprise:** Data residency and hosting location agreed contractually to meet the deployment requirements.

For Enterprise, communicate that the hosting location is determined through the agreed contract and deployment design, subject to technical feasibility, applicable law, and commercial approval.

Do not promise that data can be hosted in literally any location without restriction. Do not display the same regional limitations as Professional for Enterprise if the contract supports a different agreed location.

### Dedicated deployment
- **Essentials:** Not included.
- **Professional:** Not included.
- **Enterprise:** Dedicated deployment available, subject to agreed scope and configuration.

Do not equate dedicated deployment with a particular cloud provider, architecture, or isolation model unless that is explicitly approved.

### Data retention and exit arrangements
- **Essentials:** No customized plan-specific retention or exit arrangement.
- **Professional:** No customized plan-specific retention or exit arrangement.
- **Enterprise:** Data retention, export, and exit arrangements defined contractually.

Do not imply that customers have no rights to their data under the standard terms. The distinction is the availability of specifically agreed retention and exit arrangements. Do not invent retention periods, export formats, deletion deadlines, or transition assistance commitments.

## 4. Display and terminology rules

1. Show encryption in transit and at rest as explicitly included in all three plans.
2. Keep SSO and MFA in separate comparison rows.
3. Distinguish platform security controls from customer-facing security documentation and contractual commitments.
4. Distinguish standard security controls from custom Enterprise governance.
5. Distinguish baseline operational practices from plan-specific backup and recovery commitments.
6. Distinguish basic administrative audit trails from advanced audit trails.
7. Use consistent wording across plan cards, comparison rows, tooltips, FAQs, and related pricing-page content.
8. Use clear availability labels such as **Included**, **Available**, **By agreement**, and **Not included** where appropriate. Do not rely solely on checkmarks or crosses where a qualification is necessary.
9. Do not use absolute claims such as “fully secure,” “completely compliant,” or “guaranteed recovery.”
10. Do not add a certification badge or claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, or any other certification unless the current approved evidence explicitly supports it.

Comprehensive Learner Record for institutions
A Comprehensive Learner Record connects achievements and related evidence with provenance in an institution-governed learner record.

The institutional problem and why it matters
A transcript, individual badge and co-curricular achievement can describe different parts of a learner's experience. Connecting them should not erase who issued or assessed each item.

Architecture and how it works
A connected learner record preserves the issuer, criteria and evidence behind each achievement. The SIS and transcript retain their separately governed authoritative roles.

CertifyMe connects achievements and reviewed skills relationships in a learner-facing record with issuer provenance, supported access, correction and export workflows.

Standards and institutional workflows
CLR is a 1EdTech learner-record standard related to Open Badges and W3C credential concepts. CertifyMe's CLR 2.0 Issuer and Displayer roles are listed in the 1EdTech registry.

Registrars, academic teams and IT bring their established record and access responsibilities to a connected learner journey. Learners can present achievements without replacing the institution's official academic record.

Use cases
A university may connect academic, assessed project and co-curricular achievements. Career services may help a learner present relevant evidence while preserving issuer boundaries.

A learner record brings together a course achievement and an assessed project, preserving each award's criteria, evidence and source.

How the records are interpreted
A CLR does not automatically replace a transcript, SIS or every receiving organization's requirements. Aggregating records is not independent verification of all underlying claims.

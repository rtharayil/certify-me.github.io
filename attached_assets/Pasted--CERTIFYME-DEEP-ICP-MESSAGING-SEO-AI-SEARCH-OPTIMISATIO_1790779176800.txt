This is a **review and optimisation task**, not a request to rebuild the website.

Inspect the existing implementation first.

Review and improve these existing pages:

* `/workforce-intelligence`

# 2. PRIMARY POSITIONING PRINCIPLE

The website must not primarily speak to students.

The buyer is the institution.

The learner is an important beneficiary, but not the primary buyer for these pages.

Avoid making these pages feel like:

* B2C career websites
* Job boards
* Student portals
* Generic AI tools
* Generic analytics software
* Generic credential vendors

The positioning should communicate:

> CertifyMe provides institutional infrastructure that connects trusted credentials and learning records with skills and workforce intelligence.

# 3. PAGE 1 — WORKFORCE INTELLIGENCE

URL:

`/workforce-intelligence`

## Current positioning

The current hero is:

> Connect what your university teaches to what the workforce needs.

This is directionally strong.

Keep the underlying idea, but refine the copy so that it speaks more strongly to institutional decision-makers.

Recommended direction:

### H1

**Connect academic programmes to workforce demand.**

### Supporting copy

> CertifyMe Workforce Intelligence connects your institution's credentials and verified skills with occupations, employers and labour-market demand—helping university leaders understand programme relevance, skills gaps and career pathways.

Do not blindly use this exact copy if a better version is produced, but preserve the positioning.

# 4. WORKFORCE INTELLIGENCE — EXECUTIVE MESSAGE

Within the first viewport, the page must answer:

### Who is this for?

Universities and higher-education institutions.

### What is it?

Workforce intelligence.

### What does it connect?

Credentials + skills + programmes + occupations + employers + labour-market demand.

### Why does the institution care?

To understand programme relevance, curriculum alignment, skills gaps, employability and workforce opportunities.

### What should the visitor do?

Book a demo.

The visitor should understand all of this within approximately five seconds.

# 5. DO NOT MAKE THE WORKFORCE PAGE A JOB BOARD

Jobs can appear on the page.

However, jobs are evidence of labour-market demand.

They are NOT the primary product.

Do not use B2C language such as:

* Find your next job
* Apply now
* Find jobs for your skills
* Start your career
* Your next opportunity

Instead use:

* Employer demand
* Labour-market demand
* Occupation intelligence
* Workforce opportunities
* Career pathway intelligence
* Programme-to-career alignment
* Skills-to-occupation mapping

If a job example is shown, explain:

> This job requirement provides evidence of the skills and capabilities employers are requesting.

# 24. AI SEARCH / AEO / GEO

Do NOT implement gimmicks such as:

* keyword stuffing
* unnatural question spam
* hidden text
* AI-only pages
* fake `llms.txt` claims
* repeated keyword blocks
* fake authority statements

Google's current guidance is that the fundamentals of SEO remain the basis for visibility in AI Search experiences.

Instead:

### Make the pages easy for search systems and AI systems to understand.

For each major concept, provide:

1. Clear definition
2. Direct answer
3. Supporting explanation
4. Product implementation
5. Relevant standards
6. Internal links
7. Evidence / methodology where appropriate

Consider where appropriate:

* Organization
* WebSite
* WebPage
* BreadcrumbList
* FAQPage only where the visible page genuinely contains eligible FAQ content and current Google guidelines support the intended use

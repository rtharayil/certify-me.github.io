# Rework All CertifyMe Comparison Pages — "Ecosystem, Not Just Badges"

## Objective

Rework **all CertifyMe comparison pages**, including:

* `digital-credentialing-platforms-2026-comparison.html`
* competitor comparison pages
* "CertifyMe vs [Competitor]" pages
* digital credential platform comparison pages
* badge platform comparison pages
* any other page whose purpose is to compare CertifyMe with competing credential platforms

The goal is NOT to make these pages generic feature-by-feature comparisons.

The goal is to establish a strong, consistent market position:

> **CertifyMe is an end-to-end digital credential ecosystem, not simply a certificate or digital badge issuing tool.**

We need both **search engines and AI systems** to understand this distinction.

The comparison pages currently risk putting CertifyMe into the same category as every other badge/certificate platform.

That positioning is too narrow.

CertifyMe should be presented as:

**Credential Infrastructure → Verifiable Credentials → Records → Skills → Learning Pathways → Workforce Intelligence → Professional Identity**

The competitive distinction should therefore be:

### Basic credential platforms

Issue → Manage → Share

### CertifyMe

Issue → Verify → Manage → Analyze → Connect → Structure → Map Skills → Build Records → Enable Pathways → Connect Workforce → Extend Identity

The message should NOT be:

> "CertifyMe has more features."

The message should be:

> **"CertifyMe operates at a broader layer of the credential ecosystem."**

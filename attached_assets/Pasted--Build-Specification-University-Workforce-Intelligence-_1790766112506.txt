# Build Specification: University Workforce Intelligence & Career Opportunities

Build a production-quality, responsive web application called **Workforce Intelligence** for a university digital-credentials platform.

The purpose is NOT to create a workforce report.

The purpose is to create a **learner-facing career opportunity marketplace** where a learner's verified university credentials and skills are used to discover relevant jobs, understand why they match, identify missing skills, and apply directly.

The core product flow is:

**Open Badges 3.0 → CLR 2.0 → Skills Taxonomy → Workforce Intelligence → Job Matching → Job Application**

---

# 1. PRODUCT CONCEPT

A student/alumni member logs into the university credential platform.

They have one or more verified credentials:

* Degree
* Diploma
* Certificate
* Microcredential
* Digital badge
* Professional certification
* Course completion
* Internship
* Project
* Co-curricular achievement

Each credential contains or references verified achievement information.

The system creates a machine-readable skills profile.

Those skills are mapped to a governed skills taxonomy such as:

* ESCO
* O*NET
* NACE Career Readiness Competencies
* University custom taxonomy

The Workforce Intelligence engine then connects those skills to:

* occupations
* job roles
* employers
* active vacancies
* salary
* location
* remote/hybrid/on-site
* seniority
* employer demand
* skills demand
* career pathways

The learner can then:

**Find a job → View why they match → See skill gaps → View employer → Apply**

---

# 2. IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Do NOT design this as an analytics/reporting dashboard.

The primary CTA must be:

**Find Jobs**

The page should feel like a combination of:

* verified digital credential
* skills passport
* career marketplace
* job matching engine
* career development platform

The learner should immediately understand:

# 28. JOB MATCHING LOGIC

Initially implement a demo matching algorithm.

Example:

```text
match score =
verified skill coverage
+ skill proficiency
+ occupation relevance
+ location preference
+ experience match
+ employment preference
```

Example:

```text
Required skills = 16

Matched verified skills = 14

Base skill match = 87.5%

Location bonus = +3%

Experience bonus = +2%

Final match = 92.5%
```

Display the explanation.

Do not simply show an unexplained AI score.

---

# 29. AI EXPLANATION

Use AI only to explain the matching data.

Example:

> You are a strong match because your verified MSc credential demonstrates data analysis, SQL, statistical analysis and Python. This role additionally requests Power BI, which is not currently present in your verified skills.

The AI must not invent credentials or skills.

Every skill claim must trace back to:

**Credential → Achievement → Evidence → Skill**

```text

       ↓

       ↓

       ↓
Occupation
       ↓
Job
```

Also allow:

```text
Institution Skill
       ↓
NACE
```

and:

```text
Institution Skill
       ↓
O*NET
```

Do not hardcode the platform to one taxonomy.

Every skill should have:

```text
taxonomyName
taxonomyVersion
taxonomyId
conceptUri
mappingConfidence
mappingSource
```

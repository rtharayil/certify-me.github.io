3. CURRENT CERTIFYME SIX-LAYER MODEL
==================================================

Use the current authoritative CertifyMe six-layer model.

01 — PRESENTATION & ACCESS

Make achievement visible, branded, accessible and shareable through credential design, unique URLs, QR access, credential pages and sharing.

02 — STANDARDS & INTEROPERABILITY

Use recognised standards such as Open Badges 3.0 and W3C Verifiable Credentials for ecosystem interoperability.

03 — VERIFICATION & TRUST

Establish issuer, recipient and integrity/authenticity using cryptographic mechanisms and verification.

04 — SKILL TAXONOMY ALIGNMENT

Connect achievements to ESCO, O*NET and custom skill models where supported, turning credential information into structured, taxonomy-aligned skill claims.

05 — COMPREHENSIVE LEARNER RECORD

Bring multiple achievements together — credentials, skills, competencies, projects, experiences, assessments and learning into a connected learner record / Skill Passport.

06 — WORKFORCE INTELLIGENCE & OPPORTUNITY

Use verified skills and the complete record to understand occupations, jobs, employers, locations, workforce demand and relevant opportunities.

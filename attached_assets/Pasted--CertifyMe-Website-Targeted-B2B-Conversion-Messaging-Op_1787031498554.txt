# CertifyMe Website — Targeted B2B Conversion & Messaging Optimization

You are working on the existing CertifyMe website:

https://www.certifyme.online/

CertifyMe is a **B2B digital credential platform**. We sell the platform to **institutions and organizations**, not directly to learners.

Primary institutional audiences include:

* Universities and colleges
* Educational institutions
* Enterprises / L&D teams
* Certification bodies
* Training providers
* Professional associations
* Government organizations

Our major competitors are:

* Accredible — https://www.accredible.com/
* Credly — https://info.credly.com/
* Certifier — https://certifier.io/

## IMPORTANT: THIS IS NOT A WEBSITE REDESIGN

Do NOT rebuild the website.

Do NOT replace the current visual identity.

Do NOT create a completely new homepage.

Do NOT introduce a new design system unless the existing implementation genuinely requires it.

Do NOT remove existing product capabilities.

Do NOT change existing URLs unless absolutely necessary.

Do NOT remove existing SEO content.

Do NOT invent product features, customers, statistics, certifications or claims.

The objective is:

> **Improve the existing CertifyMe website's B2B positioning, messaging hierarchy and conversion clarity with the minimum possible structural and code changes.**

Preserve approximately 80–90% of the existing implementation wherever practical.

# 2. CORE POSITIONING

The most important correction is positioning.

CertifyMe is NOT primarily a:

* learner career app
* certificate generator
* consumer credential wallet
* job-search product

It is a:

> **B2B digital credential platform for institutions.**

The institution is the customer.

The learner is the recipient/user.

The employer is a downstream beneficiary.

Therefore, the website should primarily answer:

> "Why should my institution choose CertifyMe?"

It should then explain how learners and employers benefit.

The core institutional journey should be:

**Institution**

↓

**CertifyMe Platform**

↓

**Create / Issue / Manage / Verify / Analyze**

↓

**Learners receive trusted credentials**

↓

**Credentials become portable and useful**

↓

**Better learner / workforce outcomes**

The existing Job Intelligence / labour-market capability is valuable.

Do NOT remove it.

**Demonstrate the Workforce Relevance of Your Credentials**

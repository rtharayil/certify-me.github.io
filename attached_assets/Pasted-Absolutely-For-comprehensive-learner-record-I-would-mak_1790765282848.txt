# 21. FAQ

These are important SEO targets.

### What is a Comprehensive Learner Record?

A Comprehensive Learner Record (CLR) is a structured digital record that can bring together verified learning achievements, credentials, skills, competencies and other milestones. The 1EdTech CLR Standard provides a formal specification for interoperable learner records. ([1EdTech][1])

### What does CLR stand for?

CLR stands for **Comprehensive Learner Record**.

### What is a CLR platform?

A CLR platform helps institutions create, manage, issue, assemble, verify or exchange digital learner records and their underlying achievements.

### Does a CLR replace a transcript?

No. A CLR can complement a traditional academic transcript by representing a broader range of verified learning and achievement. ([1EdTech][2])

### What can be included in a Comprehensive Learner Record?

Depending on the implementation, a CLR can include achievements such as courses, competencies, skills, credentials and employer-based achievements or milestones. ([1EdTech][1])

### Can a CLR include digital badges?

Yes. The 1EdTech CLR Standard supports Open Badges within a broader learner record. ([1EdTech][1])

### Can a CLR include skills?

Yes. Skills and competencies can be represented within CLR implementations, including skill claims aligned with the CLR 2.0 model. ([IMS Global][4])

### Is a CLR the same as a Learning and Employment Record?

Not exactly. LER is a broader concept for digital records of learning and employment, while the CLR Standard is a formal technical specification that implements a particular form of interoperable learner record. ([1EdTech][5])

### Is CLR compatible with Verifiable Credentials?

CLR 2.0 aligns with the W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0, and 1EdTech describes CLR as compatible with the W3C VC ecosystem. ([1EdTech][1])

### Who uses Comprehensive Learner Records?

CLR technology can support universities, colleges, schools, professional bodies, employers, training providers and other organisations that issue or manage verifiable learning and achievement records.

### Can a learner have achievements from multiple organisations?

Yes. The CLR Standard supports records containing achievements from multiple issuers. ([1EdTech][1])

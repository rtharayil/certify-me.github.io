PHASE 3 — CONTENT QUALITY
==================================================

A. Does it answer the searcher's question immediately?

B. Does it contain original value?

C. Does it contain institutional/technical insight?

D. Does it contain evidence?

E. Does it contain outdated claims?

F. Does it overlap with another CertifyMe page?

G. Does it support a commercial journey?

I. Is it useful to AI systems answering the question?

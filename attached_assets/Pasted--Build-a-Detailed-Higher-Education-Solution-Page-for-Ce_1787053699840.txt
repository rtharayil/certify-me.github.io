# CRITICAL PRODUCT POSITIONING

CertifyMe is the institutional digital credential infrastructure.

SkillStory is the learner-facing experience that extends the value of those credentials into a lifelong verified skill and career identity.

The page should therefore communicate:

**University**
↓
**CertifyMe**
↓
**Degrees + Transcripts + Certificates + Badges + Achievements**
↓
**Verified Student Credential Record**
↓
**SkillStory Skill Passport**
↓
**Verified CV + Portfolio + Skills**
↓
**Career / Employer Opportunities**

Do NOT position SkillStory as a separate unrelated product.

Position it as an **extension of the institution's credential ecosystem**.

The institutional story is:

> CertifyMe helps universities issue and manage trusted digital credentials. SkillStory extends those credentials into a lifelong, verified learner identity.

This combination should be one of the major differentiators of the page.

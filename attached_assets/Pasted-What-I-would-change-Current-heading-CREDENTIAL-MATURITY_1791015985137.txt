What I would change
Current heading
CREDENTIAL MATURITY ENQUIRY

I would change this.
“Enquiry” feels like a form or sales process.
Better:
CREDENTIAL MATURITY
What Should Your Credentials Do?
Your credentials represent the achievements your institution delivers. The question is how much value they should create beyond the certificate itself.

This is more engaging and directly connects to your infrastructure story.
Recommended four-level copy
01 — ISSUE
I just need to issue certificates and awards
PDF certificates, printed documents and event awards with basic digital delivery.
Best suited for: Simple credential issuance
02 — SHARE
I want branded digital credentials recipients can share
Create branded digital badges and certificates that recipients can access, share and showcase online.
Best suited for: Digital recognition and sharing
03 — VERIFY
I want credentials that are trusted and recognised
Give credentials verifiable identity, authenticity and portability through recognised digital credential standards.
Best suited for: Trusted, portable digital credentials
This is where CertifyMe begins →
04 — CONNECT
I want credentials connected to skills, learner records and opportunity
Connect credentials with skills, competencies, learner records and workforce intelligence to create a more complete institutional record.
Best suited for: Connected credential and skills infrastructure
This is where CertifyMe becomes infrastructure →
Why I prefer this structure
The four words give the visitor an incredibly easy mental model:
01 ISSUE
↓
02 SHARE
↓
03 VERIFY
↓
04 CONNECT
And this maps very naturally to your six-layer model.
The important shift is that Level 4 isn't simply “more features.” It represents a fundamentally different institutional capability.
I would also change the bottom CTA
Current:
Not sure which level fits your program? Take the full assessment

That's okay, but slightly generic.
I'd use:
Not sure where your institution sits?
Explore the full credential maturity assessment →
Or more action-oriented:
How mature is your credential strategy?
Take the full assessment →
I prefer the second.
One important strategic change
I would remove these phrases:
“CertifyMe might not be the right fit”

“CertifyMe would be an overkill”

They are honest, but they introduce unnecessary negative framing at the bottom of your homepage.
You can communicate the same qualification without talking the visitor out of CertifyMe.
For example:
01
Best suited for simple credential issuance
02
Best suited for digital recognition and sharing
03
Best suited for trusted, portable credentials
04
Best suited for connected credential infrastructure
That feels much more like an objective maturity framework and much less like a sales funnel.

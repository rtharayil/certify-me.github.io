**How does CertifyMe pricing work?**

CertifyMe plans are designed around annual credential volume, recipient population, administrative requirements, and organizational scope. Enterprise requirements can be discussed with our team.

**What is the difference between recipients and credentials?**

A recipient is a person receiving a credential. A recipient may receive multiple credentials during the subscription year, so recipient population and annual credential issuance are different measures.

**Are students counted as administrative users?**

No. Students and other credential recipients are different from administrative users. Administrative users access the platform to manage credential programs.

**What happens if we need more credentials?**

Contact our team to discuss additional capacity and the most appropriate subscription arrangement. Additional capacity and associated charges will be agreed with the customer.

**Can departments manage their own projects?**

CertifyMe supports project-management and administrative-access capabilities. The available permissions depend on the applicable configuration and subscription arrangement.

**Does CertifyMe provide forensic audit logs?**

CertifyMe provides administrative and audit-related capabilities. Advanced forensic audit functionality and its specific scope are available according to the applicable configuration.

**Can CertifyMe integrate with our existing systems?**

CertifyMe provides API and integration capabilities for supported systems. Integration requirements depend on the systems involved, technical scope, and agreed implementation.

**Are Skills Taxonomy Mapping and Comprehensive Learner Records included?**

These capabilities form part of the broader CertifyMe ecosystem. Their availability and commercial packaging depend on the applicable product and institutional requirements.

**Can we arrange dedicated hosting or data residency?**

Hosting and data residency requirements can be discussed with our team. Available options depend on the deployment configuration and applicable agreement.

**Can we request a tailored enterprise proposal?**

Yes. Contact our team to discuss credential volumes, organizational structure, integrations, governance requirements, and implementation needs.

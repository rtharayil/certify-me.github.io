## 2. Required corrections

1. **View and retrieve issued records:** Included in all plans.
2. **Correct / edit credential details:** Included in all plans.
3. **Revoke credentials:** Available from Professional onward. Essentials must not include this entitlement.
4. **Delete credentials:** Included in all plans.
5. **Bulk deletion of credentials:** Add a new row. Available from Professional onward.
6. **Bulk modification or reissuance of credentials:** Add a new row. Available from Professional onward.
7. **Regenerate individual credential images:** Add a new row. Available in all plans.
8. **Bulk regeneration of credential images:** Add a new row. Available from Professional onward.
9. **Set and manage expiry dates:** Included in all plans.
10. **Current credential status checks:** Included in all plans.
11. **Export credential information:** Essentials includes basic export; Professional and Enterprise include advanced export.
12. **Lifecycle history and audit records:** Enterprise includes this capability subject to the agreed audit and retention scope. Professional may purchase it as an optional paid add-on. Essentials does not include it.

## 3. Important entitlement distinctions

- Keep individual credential operations distinct from their bulk equivalents.
- Do not infer that permission to delete an individual credential includes permission to delete credentials in bulk.
- Do not infer that permission to edit or reissue an individual credential includes bulk modification or reissuance.
- Keep credential deletion separate from revocation. Deletion and revocation have different effects and must not be presented as equivalent.
- The lifecycle history and audit-records add-on for Professional must use the existing approved add-on configuration and price. Do not invent a price.
- Do not imply that buying the Professional audit/history add-on automatically grants other Enterprise entitlements.
- Do not invent export formats, bulk-processing limits, image-generation limits, or retention periods.

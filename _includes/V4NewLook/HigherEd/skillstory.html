From Student Record to Career Identity

The university remains the trusted issuer at every stage. Here is how the complete journey works — from first enrolment to lifelong learner.

01
Step 1 — Learn
Student Completes a Programme

The student completes a degree, course, project, internship or institutional activity recognized by the university.

02
Step 2 — Earn
University Issues a Verified Credential

The institution issues a tamper-proof, Open Badges 3.0 certified credential through CertifyMe — automatically or on demand.

03
Step 3 — Verify
Credential Joins the Verified Record

The credential is instantly verifiable by employers, institutions or any third party — with full institutional authority maintained.

04
Step 4 — Build
Student Builds a Skill Passport

Through SkillStory, the student adds credentials, projects, skills, internships and achievements into a portable Skill Passport.

05
Step 5 — Showcase
SkillStory Creates a Verified Profile

SkillStory generates a verified CV, professional portfolio and AI-readable profile built from the student's verified institutional record.

06
Step 6 — Lifelong
Alumni Carry Their Record Forward

Long after graduation, alumni maintain access to their verified credentials. The university remains their trusted issuer for life.

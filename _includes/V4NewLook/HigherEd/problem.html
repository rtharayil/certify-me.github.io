The Problem

A PDF is not a credential. It's just a file.

Universities issue thousands of documents every year that are impossible to verify, easy to forge, and useless to employers.

✕ Employers phone the registrar to verify — taking days, not seconds
✕ Degrees and transcripts sit in email attachments with no proof of authenticity
✕ Students lose access to their records the moment they graduate
✕ Administrators spend hours processing manual transcript requests

CertifyMe makes every credential instantly verifiable, permanently accessible and connected to student skills — without changing how your teams work.

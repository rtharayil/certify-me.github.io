Not all digital credentials are equal — and the difference matters for universities.

Most "digital badge" platforms issue a PNG image with embedded metadata. That's not a Verifiable Credential. Universities need credentials built on W3C VC and CLR standards — the only formats that are cryptographically verifiable, machine-readable, and platform-independent.

What basic badge platforms sell you
An image file with a link
  • ✕ Verification only works while the issuing platform stays online
  • ✕ No cryptographic signature — can be screenshot and faked
  • ✕ Not machine-readable by ATS or employer systems
  • ✕ Tied to one credential — no full academic record
  • ✕ Requires a third-party account to verify anything

Widely sold to universities as "digital credentials." Not fit for institutional use.

What every university should require
Cryptographically signed. Independently verifiable.
  • ✓ Signed with the institution's private key — tamper-proof by design
  • ✓ Anyone can verify — no account, no platform call needed
  • ✓ Works offline — not tied to any server staying alive

  • ✓ Machine-readable by ATS, HR platforms and employer systems

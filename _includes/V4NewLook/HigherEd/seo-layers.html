Open Badges 3.0 for universities

Open Badges 3.0 structures an achievement with issuer, recipient, criteria and evidence information. Universities can use it to describe assessed skills and microcredentials, rather than relying on a badge image alone.

CertifyMe's Open Badges 3.0 Issuer certification is listed in the 1EdTech registry. Standards-based records support exchange with compatible receiving systems.

W3C Verifiable Credentials and trust

W3C Verifiable Credentials describe structured, digitally verifiable claims. A compatible verifier checks cryptographic proof, issuer information and applicable status or revocation resources. A QR code locates a record; it does not by itself prove authenticity or independently establish a learner's identity.

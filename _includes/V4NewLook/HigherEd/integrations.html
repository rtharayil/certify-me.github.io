From Graduation Data to Digital Credentials

Automate the entire credential issuance workflow — from student completion data to a verified, shareable credential — at any scale.

1
University System

Your LMS, SIS or internal system holds the student completion or achievement data.

2
Completion Trigger

When a student completes a programme, the system sends a trigger to CertifyMe via API or webhook.

3
Credential Generated

CertifyMe creates a branded, Open Badges 3.0 certified credential with the student's verified data.

4
Student Notified

The student receives an email with access to their credential — no administrative intervention required.

5
Credential Becomes Verifiable & Shareable

The credential is instantly verifiable by any third party and shareable on LinkedIn, portfolios and applications — with full analytics tracked for the institution.

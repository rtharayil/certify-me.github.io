Frequently Asked Questions

How do universities use digital credentials?

Universities use digital credentials to issue tamper-proof certificates, diplomas, badges and transcripts. Digital credentials are faster to issue than printed documents, permanently verifiable, and shareable by recipients on LinkedIn and job portals — increasing institutional visibility and learner outcomes.

Are CertifyMe credentials compliant with FERPA?

Yes. CertifyMe is FERPA compliant for US educational record requirements. The platform processes only the minimum data needed for credential issuance and gives institutions full control over student records, consistent with FERPA's requirements.

Can CertifyMe integrate with university LMS platforms?

Yes. CertifyMe integrates with Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard and other major LMS platforms. When a student completes a course or programme, the credential can be issued automatically — no manual work required from faculty or administrators.

Can a university issue Open Badges 3.0 credentials?

Yes. CertifyMe is 1EdTech independently certified for Open Badges 3.0 — the current global standard for interoperable academic credentials. Institutions issuing credentials through CertifyMe can issue OB3-compliant badges and certificates recognised by any compatible platform or employer system.

What is SkillStory and how does it relate to CertifyMe?

SkillStory is the learner-facing layer of the credential ecosystem. CertifyMe issues and manages institutional credentials. SkillStory helps students bring those credentials together with skills, projects, internships and work experience into a lifelong Skill Passport, Verified CV and professional portfolio. The two products work together — CertifyMe powers the institutional credential record; SkillStory extends its value to the learner.

How long does it take to get started?

Most institutions are issuing their first credentials within a few days of setup. Implementation timelines depend on integration complexity — simple API connections to an existing LMS typically take days, while full institutional deployments with custom branding and SSO may take a few weeks.

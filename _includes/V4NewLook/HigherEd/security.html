Common questions

How do universities use digital credentials?

To issue tamper-proof degrees, certificates, badges and transcripts — faster than print, permanently verifiable, and shareable to LinkedIn and job portals. Students get more from every credential they earn, and institutions get visibility into how those credentials are being used.

Is CertifyMe FERPA compliant?

Yes. CertifyMe processes only the minimum data needed for credential issuance and gives institutions full control over student records — consistent with FERPA requirements for US educational institutions.

Does it integrate with Canvas, Moodle or our SIS?

Yes. CertifyMe integrates with Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard and other major LMS platforms via REST API, webhooks and native integrations. When a student completes a course, the credential is issued automatically — no manual work from faculty or admin.

Are credentials Open Badges 3.0 certified?

Yes. CertifyMe is independently certified by 1EdTech for Open Badges 3.0 — the current global standard. Credentials are recognized by any compatible platform, employer system or institution globally.

What is SkillStory and how does it relate to CertifyMe?

CertifyMe manages the institution's credential record. SkillStory is the learner-facing layer — it brings together verified credentials with skills, projects and work experience into a portable Skill Passport and Verified CV students carry after graduation. The two work together as one ecosystem.

How long does it take to get started?

Most institutions issue their first credentials within a few days. Simple API connections typically take days; full deployments with custom branding and SSO take a few weeks.

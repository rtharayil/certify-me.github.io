Achievement → Credential → Standards → Verification → Skills → Learner Record → Workforce Intelligence → Opportunity

  1. 01

    Presentation & Access

    Design, credential pages, unique URLs, QR access and sharing make the achievement accessible. The visible record leads to its structured data; a QR code is access, not proof.

  2. 02

    Standards & Interoperability

    Open Badges 3.0 and the W3C VC model structure claims for compatible exchange. Confirm the implemented profile and receiver before relying on interoperability.

  3. 03

    Verification & Trust

    Verification examines issuer authority, proof integrity and applicable status. That record supplies achievement context for skill review; valid proof alone does not establish competence or independent learner identity.

  4. 04

    Skill Taxonomy Alignment

    Academic review relates outcomes and evidence to ESCO, O*NET or institutional skill frameworks. Those reviewed relationships can travel with the achievement into a learner record.

  5. 05

    Comprehensive Learner Record

    A CLR connects achievements, skills and experiences with their provenance. It complements the transcript and supplies learning context for careers teams, not a replacement SIS.

  6. 06

    Workforce Intelligence & Opportunity

    Workforce Intelligence relates reviewed skills and learner-record context to occupations, jobs and employers through the Job Engine. Opportunity insight informs decisions; it does not guarantee employment.

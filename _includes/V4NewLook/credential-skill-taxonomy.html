Skill Taxonomy Alignment

Make capability understandable to people — and AI.

A credential title says what was awarded, but not necessarily what a learner can do. CertifyMe helps institutions map achievement evidence to global and institutional skill taxonomies—making capability understandable, comparable and machine-readable while keeping your institution’s own skill language authoritative.

  • Global Taxonomies Use ESCO and O*NET as reference frameworks for broader comparison.
  • Institutional Skill Models Keep custom and programme-specific terminology authoritative.
  • Structured Skill Mapping Relate skills to achievement evidence through reviewed, machine-readable context.

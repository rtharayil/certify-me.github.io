07

Transcript security and integrity

Transcript Security & Integrity

Digital signing can provide evidence of issuer authenticity and document integrity. Identity association, access control and signature validation address distinct parts of transcript trust.

  • Issuer provenance — identify the institution responsible for the record.
  • Document integrity — check whether signed content matches its issued state.
  • Recipient access — apply institutional controls for who can access a record.

Issuer keys, applicable status information and verification resources support the record's verification lifecycle.

Governance / Retention

Academic records need clear owners and operating rules. EduTranscript by CertifyMe supports a transcript workflow that can be aligned with university governance, privacy requirements and records-management practice.

  • Define who may request and access each record.
  • Set review, approval and reissue responsibilities.
  • Align transcript availability and retention with institutional schedules.
  • Coordinate integration and security requirements with IT and records teams.

Where verification depends on issuer keys or status resources, institutions should plan for those dependencies as part of their operating model. Retention and ongoing availability follow the institution's chosen policy and service configuration.

EduTranscript keeps the university in control of the academic record and the rules around its use.

Review security and privacy practices with your IT, Registrar and records-management teams.

The transcript management problem

The Transcript Management Problem

Email requests, manual eligibility checks, document preparation, dispatch and status questions can leave Registrar and Student Services teams coordinating the same request across disconnected steps.

EduTranscript by CertifyMe gives institutions a way to bring those hand-offs into a visible workflow—while the university remains in charge of its academic records.

  • Requests and follow-ups split across inboxes
  • Repeated checks and manual processing
  • Unclear status across approval and delivery
  • Registrar time spent responding to verification enquiries

Standards & Interoperability

Build for the ecosystem, not just the platform.

A credential becomes more valuable when it is built on recognised standards rather than a proprietary format. CertifyMe uses Open Badges 3.0 and W3C Verifiable Credentials to create standards-based credentials that can participate in broader, compatible digital credential ecosystems.

  • Portability Support credential use beyond the issuing platform through compatible formats.
  • Interoperability Compatible systems can exchange and interpret credential information.
  • Ecosystem Ready Use recognised structures rather than proprietary formats.

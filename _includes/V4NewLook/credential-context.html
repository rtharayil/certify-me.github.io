A six-layer approach

A digital credential is more than what you see.

For a university, a credential can carry more than a title. Institution-governed credentials connect an achievement to compatible standards and supporting proof, related skills and learner records, and workforce context—giving the credential meaning beyond its visible certificate.

The certificate is one layer. Depending on the credential, additional context can help explain what was achieved, how it aligns, and where it may be relevant.

  • Standards & proof
  • Skills & records
  • Workforce context

A connected institutional view

What Is Workforce Intelligence?

Workforce Intelligence connects what a university teaches and assesses with the skills, occupations and employer requirements that shape the wider workforce. It gives leadership, academic, careers and employer-engagement teams a shared way to explore how programmes relate to changing demand.

Institutional evidence remains institution-defined. External market context is separately sourced and mapped, so teams can bring both into curriculum and workforce conversations without confusing their origins.

Institution-led meaning and use

Institutional Governance

Universities define what their programmes teach, what achievements mean and how evidence is used. Workforce intelligence adds a separate market perspective; it does not replace academic judgement or turn an external signal into a conclusion about a learner.

Institutional evidence

Institutions govern programme outcomes, assessment, credentials and the meaning of represented skills.

External market data

Separately sourced employer and job information is structured and mapped to skill and occupation concepts.

Reviewed relationships

More than a transcript

A transcript is one part of the learner's academic record.

Universities can connect transcripts with degrees, certificates, badges, micro-credentials, co-curricular achievements and internship records in a broader Comprehensive Learner Record. The institution remains the source of record; skills stay connected to reviewed achievements and their context.

A better handoff, both sides

Students get access. Registrars keep control.

The workflow serves the people requesting the record and the teams accountable for issuing it.

For students

A digital route to request, access and securely share institution-issued records.

  • Request records online
  • Access and download a digital transcript
  • Share with another university or employer

For registrars and administrators

Centralized controls for the work behind each approved academic record.

  • Route review and approval
  • Issue at individual or batch scale
  • Track activity with logs and reporting
  • Retain issuer authority and branding

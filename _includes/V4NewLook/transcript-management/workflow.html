The record matters beyond the file

A PDF is not a modern academic record.

Traditional paper, email and PDF workflows can leave students waiting while staff coordinate requests, approvals and delivery. Copied or altered documents are hard for recipients to assess, often leading to another call to the registrar.

Modern transcript management keeps the university in control while giving approved records a secure digital route to students and the people they choose to share them with.

LAYER 02 · STANDARDISATION

Use standards designed for credentials to travel.

Open Badges 3.0 represents achievements and evidence as portable digital badges; the W3C Verifiable Credentials data model describes claims that compatible systems can exchange and check.

These standards support international interoperability. Global acceptance is not guaranteed: recognition depends on receiving-system support and institutional policy.

Illustrative fields Issuer, achievement, recipient, evidence, status and proof concepts.

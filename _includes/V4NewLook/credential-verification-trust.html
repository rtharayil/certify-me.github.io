Verification & Trust

Don’t just show it. Prove it.

A QR code can provide access to a credential. Verification goes further: CertifyMe uses cryptographic mechanisms to check whether a credential is authentic and whether its signed data has been altered.

  • Who issued it? Check the credential’s association with its issuing institution and key.
  • Who was it issued to? Check which recipient the issuer identifies as the credential subject.
  • Has it been altered? Check the integrity of the credential’s signed data.

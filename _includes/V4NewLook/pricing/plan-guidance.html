Use these starting points to focus your comparison. Your actual capacity and requirements should guide the conversation.

Higher education

03

K–12 and schools

Review Essentials for a self-contained program; compare Professional if the program needs a department.

04

Enterprise HR and learning

Compare Professional for department-scoped operations, or discuss Enterprise when institution-wide governance is required.

05

Government and public sector

Use the Enterprise conversation to define governance, implementation, security review, and contractual requirements for your organization.

06

Training providers

Compare Essentials for a single-team program and Professional when a dedicated department is part of your operating model.

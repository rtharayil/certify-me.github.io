Lookup is not cryptographic proof.

A QR code or credential ID can open a record for lookup. That route alone does not establish cryptographic integrity. Proof depends on the credential format and its supported verification method; assess the actual credential and verification result.

Standards have specific boundaries.

Open Badges 3.0 provides a structured achievement credential model. An issuer’s Open Badges 3.0 certification is not universal certification of every credential, deployment, or connected system.

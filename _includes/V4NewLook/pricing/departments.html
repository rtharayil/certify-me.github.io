Department scope

Add department capacity without confusing it with institution-wide access.

Department count, department management, and administrator permissions are distinct parts of your operating model.

Essentials

Essentials has zero departments and does not include department-management functionality or department add-ons.

Professional

Professional includes one department. Additional departments are paid recurring add-ons quoted by sales; no add-on price is published here.

Professional department managers are assigned access to authorized department resources, not automatic access to every department. Purchasing an additional department does not grant institution-wide administration. Confirm roles, resource boundaries and implementation responsibilities when arranging your subscription.

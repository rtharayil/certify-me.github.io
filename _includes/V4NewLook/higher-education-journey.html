Connected credential value across your university

CertifyMe connects academic achievement, institutional records and career relevance while preserving each team's authority. A shared credential foundation gives university teams a consistent way to recognise learning and present its value.

Registrar: award and record governance

Digital diplomas and certificates and digital transcripts carry university-approved award information into accessible records. Verification supports receiving teams, while a Comprehensive Learner Record adds broader achievement context alongside the authoritative transcript and SIS.

Provost and academic leadership: programme meaning

Academic leadership retains authority over outcomes and assessment. Reviewed outcome-to-skill relationships make learning more interpretable, and microcredentials recognise focused achievements with programme-defined criteria and evidence.

Dean and faculty: achievement evidence

Digital badges give assessed projects and programme milestones a shareable presentation. Open Badges achievement structures retain the issuer, criteria and evidence behind that presentation, with confidential feedback governed by institutional access rules.

CIO and IT: supported interfaces and continuity

The credential lifecycle, APIs and supported integration options connect credential operations with institutional systems. The W3C issuer–holder–verifier model provides a foundation for structured exchange; compatibility depends on the selected interfaces and receiving systems.

IT/security: assurance, privacy and residency

Enterprise SSO and MFA, access controls and agreed data-processing terms support the university's security requirements. Security and deployment information describes the assurance and hosting scope relevant to institutional credential operations.

Career Services: learner presentation and opportunity research

A connected learner record and Skill Passport help learners present assessed achievements with issuer provenance. Occupation and employer-demand context supports career conversations and opportunity research alongside that learning evidence.

Programme teams: visibility into credential engagement

Credential analytics provides visibility into available issuance and engagement events. Academic and employment outcomes remain separate measures. The institutional case studies show how customers have applied CertifyMe within their own programmes.

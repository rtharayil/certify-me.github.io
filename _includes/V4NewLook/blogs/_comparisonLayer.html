Credential journey

Issuance endpoint or ecosystem starting point?

Issuance-first model

Design → issue → share → report

  1. Create and distribute a certificate or badge.
  2. Host a recipient page and sharing experience.
  3. Track delivery, views, shares, or engagement.

A practical fit when the programme’s objective ends with efficient distribution and recognition.

CertifyMe ecosystem model

Issue → accumulate → interpret → activate

  1. Issue a signed, standards-based credential.
  2. Accumulate evidence through records, skills, and pathways.
  3. Connect verified skills to current workforce intelligence.
  4. Carry achievements into a reusable professional identity.

Designed for programmes where the credential must keep creating value after it is awarded.

Credential maturity

Level 3 Trust and Level 4 Intelligence

CertifyMe positions the ecosystem as two connected layers rather than a longer badge feature list.

Level 3

Trust

Standards-based, tamper-evident credentials with cryptographic proof and independently tested Open Badges 3.0 conformance.

Independent verification still depends on resolvable issuer keys and applicable status or revocation resources.

Level 4

Intelligence

Credentials connected to CLR, skills taxonomies, learning pathways, labour-market signals, and reusable professional identity.

This layer helps issuers improve programmes and helps earners understand what their verified skills can unlock.

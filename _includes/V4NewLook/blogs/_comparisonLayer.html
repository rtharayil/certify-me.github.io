Credential maturity

Level 3 Trust and Level 4 Intelligence

CertifyMe frames the ecosystem as two connected layers.

Level 3

Trust

Standards-based, tamper-evident credentials with cryptographic proof and independently tested Open Badges 3.0 conformance.

Independent verification still depends on resolvable issuer keys and applicable status or revocation resources.

Level 4

Intelligence

Credential evidence can be connected to skills, pathways, labour-market signals, and professional identity.

Availability depends on the issuer’s programme configuration and relevant data sources.

Workforce Intelligence

Turn learning into opportunity.

Connect credentials to reviewed skills, workforce context and career pathways in one institution-facing offering. By relating learning and skill evidence to role and employer context, CertifyMe helps university teams inform careers guidance, curriculum conversations and employer engagement—while academic judgement and evidence provenance remain authoritative.

  • Job Insights Explore role, industry and location context to inform careers guidance.
  • Employer & Hiring Insights Understand employer needs as useful context for institutional engagement.
  • Personalised Pathways Use reviewed skills and learner-record context to consider possible career pathways.
  • Application Pathways Connect guidance with relevant employer application routes where opportunities or provider support exist.

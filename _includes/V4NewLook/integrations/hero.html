What should the {{page.customText}} credential workflow do?

Use an approved event in {{page.customText}} to start a credential workflow in CertifyMe. Your institution remains responsible for the award decision, recipient information and achievement criteria.

Choose the trigger and credential action

Check the current Zapier app pair for supported triggers, actions and account requirements. A course completion, form response or updated record is useful only when it represents an award your institution has approved.

Map and protect recipient information

Map the recipient, credential template and achievement fields required by the selected action. Share only necessary data and confirm permissions, access controls and applicable privacy responsibilities.

Test delivery, verification and exceptions

Run a sample with authorised test data. Check the credential's issuer, evidence and verification link, then test missing fields, duplicate events and failed deliveries before enabling the workflow.

Keep the scope clear

Availability depends on the current connector, supported action and plans. This Zapier workflow does not by itself establish accreditation, regulatory compliance, learner competence or employment outcomes.

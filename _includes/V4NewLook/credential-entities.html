Credential concepts and their relationships

CertifyMe provides digital credential infrastructure. It connects authorized credentials and verification resources with reviewed skills, learner records and separately sourced workforce context. Open Badges and CLR are achievement profiles; the W3C VC model describes claims; a Skill Passport presents the learner's evidence. None of these steps independently establishes identity or employment outcomes.

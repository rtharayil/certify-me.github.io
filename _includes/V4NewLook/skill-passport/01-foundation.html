01 · The learner-facing record

What Is a Skill Passport?

A Skill Passport brings learning and achievement into one connected record the learner can carry forward. It makes room for institution-issued credentials alongside skills, projects, experiences and learner-added evidence—without losing the source or status of each item.

The institution recognises achievement. The learner makes it part of a continuing story.

02 · More of the learner story

Transcript vs Skill Passport

A transcript remains the academic record. A Skill Passport adds context around learning and achievement, so the learner can present more than courses and grades.

Academic record

Transcript

  • Courses and modules
  • Grades and academic progress
  • Qualifications awarded
+
Learner-facing record

Skill Passport

  • Credentials and verified skills
  • Projects, experiences and evidence
  • Achievements, with source and status

The two records have different jobs. A Skill Passport can bring academic and broader evidence into a learner-controlled view.

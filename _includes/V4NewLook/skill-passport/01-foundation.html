Beyond the transcript

A transcript records part of the journey. A Skill Passport connects the whole journey.

The transcript remains an authoritative academic record. The Skill Passport extends the learner record by representing broader evidence of what the learner has achieved and demonstrated.

An institution keeps its role as issuer and attestor. Learners gain a richer, portable view of the achievement behind each credential.

The connected learner record

A Skill Passport is more than a certificate wallet.

It is the learner-facing record that brings issuer-backed credentials together with skills, projects, experiences, achievements and evidence—then gives the learner a way to carry and share that record over time.

Your institution recognises achievement. The learner can make that achievement part of a continuing story.

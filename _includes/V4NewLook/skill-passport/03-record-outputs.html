Evidence that can travel

A CV supported by verified learner evidence.

A Skill Passport-powered CV can draw on credentials, attested achievements, skills and evidence in the learner record. Learners retain an editable CV and decide how to present their experience for an application.

SKILL PASSPORT→CV→PORTFOLIO→APPLICATION

The record can substantiate selected CV content; it does not automatically verify every CV field.

Portability by design

Built to travel with the learner.

Standards-based digital credentials and learner records help institutions issue and learners carry structured achievement. CLR 2.0, Open Badges 3.0 and W3C Verifiable Credentials play distinct roles in this standards landscape.

Portability helps an achievement retain its structure and provenance as it moves beyond the issuing institution. The right system connections and implementation scope are established with each institution.

Trust beyond the learner

Anyone should be able to verify the record.

The value of a Skill Passport is not only that the learner can show it. A recipient can open a public achievement verification link and review who attested, their role, the attestation date and record integrity—without a viewer account.

08 · A chosen view for each audience

Portfolio / CV

A learner can select relevant credentials, skills, projects and experiences to shape a portfolio or CV for a particular purpose. The Skill Passport is the connected record; the CV and portfolio are ways to present selected parts of it.

Connected record→Selected items→Portfolio, CV or application

The learner decides what to present. Each item keeps its own source and verification status.

09 · Trust at the level of each item

Verification

A recipient can inspect the source and verification context of a credential or attested achievement. Institution-issued credentials retain their issuer; learner-added entries remain learner-added unless a separate review attests them.

Open shared record→Select an item→Review source and status

Institution-issued · issued by its named institution

Learner-added · provided by the learner

Attested · reviewed and confirmed by an attestor

Verification is item-level. Adding an item does not make it institution-verified.

Layer 5

Comprehensive Learner Record

Connect the learner’s achievements.

Bring credentials, skills, competencies and learning experiences together in a structured, lifelong Comprehensive Learner Record (CLR), while maintaining institutional governance and issuer context. The CLR complements the academic transcript; it does not replace it. Opportunity context can support future uses of the record without changing its authoritative source.

  • For Universities A connected view of learning, skills and achievements, with institutional oversight.
  • For Schools Capture and recognise the breadth of student achievements.
  • For Learners A shareable record to showcase learning and achievements.
  • For Employers Structured achievement information to support interpretation of candidate capabilities.

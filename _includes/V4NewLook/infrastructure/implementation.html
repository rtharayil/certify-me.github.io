Custom development starts with an agreed solution design.

Where your use case needs more than platform configuration, discovery defines the high-level solution before development is scoped. Institutional priorities determine the source systems, data boundaries, connection methods and six-layer capabilities to support.

Source connections & data contracts

Identify authoritative records, learner references and permitted fields. Agree the API, event or file-based exchange suited to the source system, including access permissions, data validation and how exceptions will be handled.

Layers 01–03 · Access, standards & trust

Institution-specific award workflows

Define award eligibility, approval responsibilities and issuance triggers. Scope the configuration or custom extension needed to connect those decisions to credential distribution, verification and lifecycle management.

Layers 01 & 03 · Access & trust

Connected skills, records & context

Agree reviewed outcome-to-skill relationships and how achievements form a learner record. Preserve each award’s provenance while defining how separately sourced workforce information adds context to institutional learning evidence.

Layers 04–06 · Skills, learner record & workforce

Your institution brings the context

Institutional goals, system owners, award policies, approved data sources, security and privacy requirements, and the people who will govern the work.

CertifyMe works through the agreed scope

Architecture discussion, platform configuration and scoped development or integration work are shaped around the source systems and workflows identified together. The connection method and scope depend on the institutional context.

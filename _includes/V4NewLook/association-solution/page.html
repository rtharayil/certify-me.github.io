The distinction

A Digital Badge Is More Than a Badge

A badge can recognize an achievement. A credential can provide verifiable evidence of that achievement. Connect credentials to skills and a professional record, and they can contribute to a portable picture of capability.

The issuing association defines what an achievement means. Structured mappings help describe it; they do not independently prove competence.

BADGE Achievement recognized
CREDENTIAL Verified achievement from an identified issuer
SKILL Capability represented through a reviewed mapping
LEARNER / PROFESSIONAL RECORD Connected history across programs
PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY Portable evidence of capability

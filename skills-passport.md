  - question: "How does the CertifyMe learner wallet work?"
    answer: "When a learner earns a credential through any CertifyMe-powered institution, it automatically appears in their skills passport wallet. The wallet is a personal, permanent profile that the learner owns and controls. They can share a link to their full passport or to individual credentials for employers, LinkedIn, or applications."
